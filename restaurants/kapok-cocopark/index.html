Kapok (木棉·初恋咕噜肉)

COCO Park 店

Shopping Park, Futian · Shenzhen

CuisineGuangzhou home cooking · 广州菜
Price¥110–160 per person
2No. 2 Guangzhou Restaurant in FutianDianping 好评榜
4.6Dianping 4.6 / 59,564 reviews at the time of listing

Guangzhou home cooking in a shopping mall, with a sweet-and-sour pork that has its own fan club.

木棉 is a Guangzhou-cuisine restaurant on the fourth floor of COCO Park in Futian — six years old, busy every night, and priced so that a family can eat without thinking about it: ¥112 a head.

The menu is Guangzhou home cooking with a bit of theatre. The signature is 初恋咕噜肉, sweet-and-sour pork served on a hot stone so the sauce keeps caramelising as you eat it. The pigeon arrives with rice straw and is lit at the table. The chicken is smoked over Longjing tea.

It is a mall restaurant, and it behaves like one — queues, a buzzer, tables turned quickly — but the cooking is not mall food. The vegetables get as much attention as the meat: lard-fried choy sum, salted egg yolk with loofah, plain blanched greens.

One quirk worth knowing: the Dianping list includes the same smoked chicken twice, at No. 2 and No. 7. Order it once.

Top 10 must-try

Dianping 推荐菜, TOP 1–10 for the COCO Park branch — ranked by the app's like counts.

Source: Dianping 推荐菜 · TOP 1–10

  1. 初恋咕噜肉
    First-Love Sweet-and-Sour Pork ¥88 No. 1

    The dish the restaurant is named after: pork in a fruit-sweet sauce, served on a hot stone so it keeps caramelising at the table.

  2. 龙井烟熏太爷鸡
    Longjing Tea-Smoked Chicken ¥98 No. 2

    Smoked over tea leaves — the smoke is light, the tea is what stays. Half a bird is enough for three.

  3. 厚切岩烧牛舌
    Thick-Cut Stone-Grilled Beef Tongue ¥138 Grill

    Cut thick, grilled on stone, still pink in the middle. The most expensive thing in the ten.

  4. 禾秆草烧乳鸽
    Straw-Fired Pigeon ¥58 Theatre

    Brought to the table with rice straw, then lit with a torch in front of you. The smoke is the point; the pigeon is good too.

  5. 猪油渣炒菜心
    Choy Sum Fried with Lard Croutons ¥48 Vegetable

    Bitter greens, crisp lard, wok heat. The vegetable that makes the table feel like a Cantonese home.

  6. 煲仔金沙焗丝瓜
    Claypot Salted-Egg Yolk Loofah ¥58 Vegetable

    Salted egg yolk clinging to loofah in a claypot. Rich and sweet at once — order it with the smoked chicken.

  7. 龙井烟熏鸡
    Longjing Smoked Chicken (short name) Duplicate

    The app lists this twice — same dish as No. 2 under its shorter name. Order one, not both.

  8. 紫苏啫啫跳跳蛙
    Perilla Sizzling Frog ¥58 Sizzling

    Frog in a perilla-heavy sauce, served still bubbling. Loud, garlicky, and the dish the regulars fight over.

  9. 盐水菜心
    Salted-Water Choy Sum ¥48 Vegetable

    The plain version: greens blanched in salted water, nothing else. Order it after the fried dishes.

  10. 手工冰镇鲍鱼
    Hand-Made Iced Abalone ¥68 Cold

    Abalone served over ice — firm, clean, plated like a small sculpture. A cold dish between the hot ones.

Prices from the Dianping listing. The set menus are good value: ¥238 for the autumn two-to-three-person set and ¥904 for the six-person banquet. The 300-yuan voucher sells for ¥289.
