Xinxing Restaurant (新兴饭店·非遗粤菜·老字号早茶)

北京路天字码头店

Beijing Road, Yuexiu · Guangzhou

CuisineCantonese old brand · 广州菜・老字号
Price¥90–130 per person

A 116-year-old Guangzhou brand, still cooking mutton and roast goose the old way.

新兴饭店 has been on Beijing Road for a very long time — its own banner counts 116 years, which puts its founding in the 1910s — and it holds intangible-heritage status for its Cantonese cooking, specifically the mutton dishes.

The menu has two poles. One is mutton: 焖羊 braised until it comes off the bone, 羊杂汤 in a milky broth, and a banquet version with abalone at ¥538. The other is classic Cantonese — glass-skin roast goose, white-cut chicken, steamed fish, and old dim sum like the 榄粉果 that carries the heritage label.

It also does morning tea from 08:00, which is why locals treat it as two restaurants: a yum cha room in the morning, a mutton house at night. The 柚子叉烧餐包 and the baked goat milk belong to the morning; the goose and the claypot belong to the evening.

It is No. 2 in Beijing Road for repeat customers — behind only one other room — which for a 116-year-old address is the metric that matters.

What the city, the guides and the queues actually agree on.

Top 10 must-try

Dianping 推荐菜, TOP 1–10 for the Beijing Road Tianzi Pier branch — ranked by the app's like counts.

Source: Dianping 推荐菜 · TOP 1–10

  1. 酥皮焗羊奶
    Puff-Pastry Baked Goat Milk ¥23 No. 1

    The top order and the cheapest thing here: hot goat milk under a pastry lid. Sweet, faintly gamey, unlike anything else on the menu.

  2. 玻璃脆皮烧鹅
    Glass-Skin Roast Goose ¥198 No. 2

    The other signature — skin thin enough to see through, fat rendered to a lacquer. Order a quarter for two.

  3. 非遗娥姐榄粉果
    Heritage Olive-Shaped Dumpling ¥20 No. 3

    The dim sum that carries the intangible-heritage label: translucent wrapper, pork and vegetable filling, steamed to order.

  4. 新兴秘制鲍鱼焖羊
    Secret-Recipe Braised Mutton with Abalone ¥538 No. 4

    The banquet version: mutton braised until it falls off the bone, abalone added for sweetness. Order it for four or more.

  5. 新兴秘制焖羊
    Secret-Recipe Braised Mutton ¥178 No. 5

    The dish the restaurant is famous for. Soft enough to pull apart, no gamey edge, sauce thick enough to hold rice.

  6. 柚子叉烧餐包10只
    Pomelo Char Siu Buns (10) ¥23 No. 6

    Ten small buns with a pomelo-sweet char siu filling. The thing to order with morning tea.

  7. 风味羊杂汤
    Mutton Offal Soup ¥19 No. 7

    Milky broth, offal and pickled vegetables. The cheap bowl that explains why the mutton is good.

  8. 鲜花椒蒸无骨鲫鱼
    Steamed Boneless Crucian Carp with Sichuan Pepper ¥128 No. 8

    One fish, two ways: meat steamed, bones fried. The pepper is there to cut the freshwater taste.

  9. 石烹金汤花胶翅
    Stone-Cooked Fish Maw in Golden Broth ¥68 No. 9

    Fish maw in a pumpkin-gold stock, served in a hot stone bowl. Order it as the soup course.

  10. 葱油淋白切鸡
    White-Cut Chicken with Scallion Oil ¥78 No. 10

    Poached chicken, chilled, scallion oil poured over. The control order against the heavier mutton dishes.

Prices from the Dianping listing. The sets are good value: ¥153 for the two-person classic dim sum, ¥230 for the Canton Fair selection, and ¥460 for the four-person 'Qing dynasty merchant' banquet. The 100-yuan voucher sells for ¥90.
