Xi Shi Gun (喜势滚·花胶鸡海鲜火锅)

天河店

Zhujiang New Town, Tianhe · Guangzhou

CuisineFish maw chicken and seafood hot pot · 海鲜火锅・花胶鸡
Price¥280–450 per person
锋Fengwei Treasure List 2026锋味宝藏榜 2026 上榜餐厅
4.9Dianping 4.9 / 52,181 reviews at the time of listing
荐Locals Recommend1,643 local reviews — 当地人推荐

Golden fish-maw chicken broth, luxury add-ons, and a kitchen that stays open until 4am.

喜势滚 does one thing with conviction: a golden fish-maw and chicken broth, simmered until it is thick enough to coat a spoon, then used as the base for everything else. It has been on the Fengwei Treasure List for 2026 and holds 4.9 across 2,181 reviews, with 1,643 of those from locals.

The format is luxury hot pot. You start with the small pot at ¥188 — fish maw, sea cucumber, half a chicken — drink a bowl of the broth before anything goes in, and then add what you like: live grouper, Thai mantis shrimp, A5 wagyu, palm-sized oysters at ¥38 each.

The prices climb fast if you let them: the large pot is ¥588, the leopard grouper ¥688 by weight, the wagyu ¥568. But the ¥38 oysters and the ¥38 osmanthus dessert keep a table of four within reach of ¥280 a head.

It stays open until four in the morning, which in Zhujiang New Town is a genuine service. Book for late — the room is busiest after ten.

Top 10 must-try

Dianping 推荐菜, TOP 1–10 for the Tianhe branch — ranked by the app's like counts.

Source: Dianping 推荐菜 · TOP 1–10

  1. 5A雪花和牛
    A5 Snowflake Wagyu ¥568 No. 1

    The top order: A5 wagyu, sliced thin, cooked for seconds in the broth. Marbling like a print — do not leave it in.

  2. 蟹皇花胶鱼翅辽参汤
    Crab Roe, Fish Maw, Fin and Sea Cucumber Soup ¥268 No. 2

    A pot built on three expensive things — fish maw, fin and sea cucumber — with crab roe for sweetness. Ask what is in it that day.

  3. 泰国濑尿虾
    Thai Mantis Shrimp ¥168 No. 3

    Live mantis shrimp, split and cooked at the table. The cheapest luxury on the list and the one to order first.

  4. 游水大东星斑
    Live Leopard Coral Grouper ¥688 No. 4

    Whole live grouper, sliced and poached for three seconds a piece. Priced by weight — confirm before it leaves the tank.

  5. 养颜花胶辽参葵花鸡锅
    Fish Maw, Sea Cucumber and Sunflower Chicken Pot ¥188 No. 5

    The house pot in its smaller form: golden broth, fish maw, sea cucumber, half a chicken. This is the order that makes the restaurant.

  6. 游水老鼠斑
    Live Humpback Grouper ¥288 No. 6

    The connoisseur's fish — fewer bones, richer skin. Cheaper than the leopard grouper and, to some, better.

  7. 深海乳山蚝
    Deep-Sea Rushan Oyster ¥38 No. 7

    A palm-sized oyster poached in the broth until it just firms. At ¥38, order four.

  8. 桂花雪燕羹
    Osmanthus Snow Bird's Nest Soup ¥38 No. 8

    Sweet, cold, slippery — the dessert that resets the palate after a rich pot. The cheapest thing on the list.

  9. 花胶鱼翅辽参葵花鸡锅
    Large Fish Maw, Fin, Sea Cucumber and Chicken Pot ¥588 No. 9

    The same pot at banquet size, with more of everything. Order it for four or more; it is the reason groups book.

  10. 花胶鸡
    Fish Maw Chicken Pot No. 10

    The base pot — golden, collagen-thick, and the thing to drink a bowl of before anything goes in. Ask for the price by size.

Prices from the Dianping listing. Live fish is priced by weight — confirm before it leaves the tank. The 100-yuan voucher sells for ¥80; the ¥562 set feeds two to three.
