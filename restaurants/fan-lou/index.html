Fan Lou (蘩楼)

黄贝岭店

Luohu · Shenzhen

CuisineCantonese teahouse / dim sum · 粵式茶樓・點心
Price¥90–150 per person
必Dianping Must-Eat ListSix consecutive years at Huangbeiling
老Revived Xiguan brandLate-Qing teahouse lineage

A Xiguan teahouse rebuilt in Shenzhen — six straight years on the Must-Eat List.

蘩楼 began in late-Qing Guangzhou, in Xiguan, founded by a chef surnamed 黄. Its reputation rested on one detail: 虾饺 with a wrapper thin enough to read through, folded around shrimp that had been bought that morning. The original house did not survive the twentieth century.

The brand was rebuilt in Shenzhen, where it now runs some of the busiest teahouse floors in the city. The Huangbeiling room has appeared on the 大众点评必吃榜 for six consecutive years — an unusually long run for a dim sum house in a city that opens new ones every month.

It has since expanded to Shanghai, including a branch at 徐家汇汇金百货. That is precisely the case this site was built for: one flagship post in Shenzhen, one line in Shanghai, and a link back here rather than a second competing review.

A teahouse is a room, a trolley and a pot of tea — everything else is detail.The Xiguan teahouse tradition 蘩楼 is reviving

Why it became famous

What the city, the guides and the queues actually agree on.

  • ◆香脆明虾红米肠 — the crisp shrimp rice-roll that most photographs of the brand are built around.
  • ◆Hand-folded 虾饺 and a full trolley service in a Lingnan-style room.
  • ◆Six consecutive Dianping Must-Eat years at the Huangbeiling branch.
  • ◆Now in Shanghai as well, which makes it the test case for the branch-reference system on this site.

Signatures worth ordering

Three dishes the house is known for; the full top ten is being researched.

Why only three: this post is on the list but has not been written up yet. Rather than invent a top ten, the page shows the dishes that are consistently cited — the full ranking lands when the feature is written.
  1. 香脆明虾红米肠
    Crispy Shrimp Rice-Noodle Roll Known signature

    Three textures in one bite: soft red rice wrapper, crisp middle, whole shrimp.

  2. 蘩楼露笋虾皇饺
    Asparagus Shrimp Dumpling Known signature

    The house benchmark — order it before anything else.

  3. 金牌脆烤乳鸽
    Golden Crisp Roast Pigeon Known signature

    Roasted to order, split at the table. Best shared between two.

  4. 干炒牛河
    Beef Ho Fun, Dry-Fried Known signature

    Wok hei test. If the noodles arrive glossy without pooling oil, the kitchen is awake.
