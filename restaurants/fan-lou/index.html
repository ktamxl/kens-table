Fan Lou (蘩楼)

蛇口大厦店

Shekou, Nanshan · Shenzhen

CuisineCantonese teahouse / dim sum · 粤式茶楼・点心
Price¥80–120 per person
必Dianping Must-Eat List2026 — eight consecutive years for the brand
1No. 1 Cantonese Restaurant in ShekouDianping 回头客榜 — repeat customers
4.8Dianping 4.8 / 511,769 reviews at the time of listing

A Xiguan teahouse rebuilt in Shenzhen — eight straight years on the Must-Eat List.

蘩楼 began in late-Qing Guangzhou, in Xiguan, founded by a chef surnamed 黄. Its reputation rested on one detail: 虾饺 with a wrapper thin enough to read through, folded around shrimp that had been bought that morning. The original house did not survive the twentieth century.

The brand was rebuilt in Shenzhen, where it now runs some of the busiest teahouse floors in the city, and it has been on the 大众点评必吃榜 for eight consecutive years — an unusually long run for a dim sum house in a city that opens new ones every month.

This is the Shekou Dasha room: 08:00 to 16:00 for yum cha, then 17:00 to 20:30 for dinner, a queue at the door by nine in the morning, and a trolley service that has not been modernised into an app. The 蛇口 room is currently No. 1 in the district for repeat customers, which for a teahouse is a better metric than any award.

It has also expanded to Shanghai, including a branch at 徐家汇汇金百货 — the case this site was built for: one flagship post, one line per other city, and a link back here rather than a second competing review.

Why it became famous

What the city, the guides and the queues actually agree on.

  • ◆香脆明虾红米肠 — the crisp shrimp rice-roll that most photographs of the brand are built around.
  • ◆Hand-folded 虾饺 and a full trolley service in a Lingnan-style room.
  • ◆Eight consecutive Dianping Must-Eat years for the brand.
  • ◆No. 1 in Shekou for repeat customers — locals come back, not just visitors.

Top 10 must-try

Dianping 推荐菜, TOP 1–10 for the Shekou Dasha branch — ranked by the app's like counts. The menu is the same across the chain.

Source: Dianping 推荐菜 · TOP 1–10

  1. 香脆明虾红米肠
    Crispy Shrimp Rice-Noodle Roll ¥33 No. 1

    Three textures in one bite: soft red rice wrapper, crisp middle, whole shrimp. The most-ordered item in the shop.

  2. 蘩楼露笋虾饺皇
    Fan Lou Asparagus Shrimp Dumpling ¥36 No. 2

    The house benchmark — wrapper thin enough to read through, asparagus for crunch. Order it before anything else.

  3. XO酱山椒蒸凤爪
    Chicken Feet with XO Sauce and Sichuan Pepper ¥36 No. 3

    Steamed until the skin gives way, then hit with XO and a numbing edge. The best version of the dish in Shenzhen.

  4. 广州荔湾艇仔粥
    Guangzhou Liwan Sampan Congee ¥33 No. 4

    Rice congee with peanuts, squid and pork skin — the old Liwan garnish, not a simplified version.

  5. 金枕飘香榴莲酥
    Durian Pastry ¥33 No. 5

    Warm durian in a flaky shell. Order it at the start, not the end — it is better hot and it takes time.

  6. 豉汁蒸新鲜排骨
    Steamed Pork Ribs in Black-Bean Sauce ¥36 No. 6

    Small, fatty ribs made for congee. The dish that tells you whether the steamer is running properly.

  7. 干炒牛河
    Beef Ho Fun, Dry-Fried ¥38 No. 7

    Wok hei test. If the noodles arrive glossy without pooling oil, the kitchen is awake.

  8. 绵香沙爹金钱肚
    Satay Beef Tripe ¥45 No. 8

    Thick-cut tripe, braised soft, finished in satay. The most expensive dim sum on the list, and worth it.

  9. 传统手工炸油条
    Hand-Made Fried Dough Stick ¥19 No. 9

    Hollow, crisp, made to order. Tear it into the congee or eat it with the soy.

  10. 凉拌北海墨鱼仔
    Chilled Beihai Baby Cuttlefish ¥36 No. 10

    Cold, crunchy, sour-hot dressing. The palate reset between the fried courses.

Prices from the Dianping listing for the Shekou Dasha branch. The set menus are the value: ¥201 for the two-person 蘩楼必吃节 set and ¥470 for six. The 100-yuan voucher sells for ¥70 in the app.
