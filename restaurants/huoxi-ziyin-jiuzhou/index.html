Seafood hot pot in Nanshan Science Park, with a deboned pigeon as the signature.

火玺·紫隐九州 is a high-end seafood hot pot room on the ground floor of the Jiuzhou Electronics Building in Nanshan Science Park — four years old, private rooms, a phone booking line, and a menu that assumes a company card.

The signature is 仙鹤神针: a deboned pigeon stuffed, braised in abalone sauce, then cut open at the table so the juice runs into the bowl. It is an old Cantonese banquet dish, and it is the reason the restaurant ranks No. 1 for seafood hot pot in the district.

Around it the menu is built on two pots — a lobster broth that gets turned into rice at the end, and a conch, fish maw and chicken broth that is the cheaper, deeper option. The crab dishes are the theatre: one arrives inside a white-chocolate egg and is set alight with baijiu.

It is not a cheap meal — ¥469 a head before the ¥1,098 sashimi platter — but the ¥48 tofu and the free rice at the end of the lobster pot keep it from being only that.

Top 10 must-try

Dianping 推荐菜, TOP 1–10 for the Science Park branch — ranked by the app's like counts.

Source: Dianping 推荐菜 · TOP 1–10

  1. 仙鹤神针
    Crane's Needle — Deboned Pigeon Stuffed with Fin ¥288 No. 1

    The house signature: pigeon deboned, stuffed, braised in abalone sauce and cut open at the table so the juice runs out. Ask whether the stuffing is fin or fish maw that day.

  2. 海鲜火锅
    Seafood Hot Pot Hot pot

    The base order: fish maw, grouper, and a pot of clear broth. Priced by what you add — ask before it goes in.

  3. 招牌竹笙龙虾汤锅
    Signature Lobster Broth with Bamboo Fungus ¥398 Hot pot

    Lobster stock, bamboo fungus, and the leftover broth turned into a rice pot at the end for free. The best value on the menu.

  4. 黄金松叶蟹斗
    Golden Spider Crab Shell ¥688 Showpiece

    A whole crab presented inside a white-chocolate egg, flambéed with baijiu at the table. Theatre, but the crab underneath is good.

  5. 火玺螺头花胶鸡锅汤
    Huoxi Conch, Fish Maw and Chicken Broth ¥308 Hot pot

    The other pot: conch for savoury depth, fish maw for body, chicken underneath. Golden and thick — order it if you are not having lobster.

  6. 黑松露小豆腐
    Black Truffle Tofu ¥48 Snack

    Crisp outside, soft inside, truffle on the finish. The cheap order that everyone at the table reaches for.

  7. 椒椒椒椒蟹
    Four-Pepper Crab ¥368 Crab

    Crab in a pepper-heavy sauce with rice cakes underneath. The rice cakes soak up everything the crab leaves.

  8. 级牛小排
    Premium Beef Short Rib ¥398 Beef

    Marbled short rib for the pot — sliced thin, cooked for seconds. Order it if the table is still hungry after the seafood.

  9. 招牌堂捞膏蟹粉丝
    Signature Roe Crab with Glass Noodles ¥688 Crab

    Crab roe and glass noodles tossed at the table. The noodles are the point; the crab is what flavours them.

  10. 特级刺身拼盘
    Premium Sashimi Platter ¥1,098 Raw

    Salmon, toro and foie gras on ice. The most expensive thing here, and the one for a celebration rather than a Tuesday.

Prices from the Dianping listing. The hot pot is priced by what you add, so the bill moves — confirm before the seafood goes in. The 100-yuan voucher sells for ¥81.
