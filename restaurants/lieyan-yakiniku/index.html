Lieyan Yakiniku (烈焰·纯血和牛烧肉)

黑金店

Zhujiang New Town, Tianhe · Guangzhou

CuisineJapanese yakiniku / wagyu · 日式烧肉・和牛
Price¥390–700 per person
2No. 2 Yakiniku Restaurant in Zhujiang New TownDianping 口味榜
4.8Dianping 4.8 / 52,682 reviews at the time of listing
美Dianping 漂亮饭Listed for presentation as well as food

Australian pure-blood wagyu, grilled at the table, from ¥58 fried rice to a ¥1,988 golden bull.

烈焰 is a wagyu yakiniku room on the second floor of the Kaihua International Centre, next to the Ritz-Carlton in Zhujiang New Town. Four years old, private rooms, free parking, and open until midnight — which in this district is the real luxury.

The beef is Australian pure-blood wagyu, and the menu is built around cuts rather than courses: thick-cut marbled rib, short rib, outside skirt, ribeye. The grill is at the table and the staff will cook the first piece for you, which is worth accepting.

The pricing is deliberately wide. 和牛炒饭 at ¥58 and a ¥198 wagyu flower make it possible to eat here without spending ¥400; the 至尊金牛 at ¥1,988 is a platter on a golden bull, ordered for birthdays and photographed by everyone in the room.

It is No. 2 in Zhujiang New Town for yakiniku taste, with 4.8 across 2,682 reviews. Book for dinner — the room fills after eight.

Top 10 must-try

Dianping 推荐菜, TOP 1–10 for the Black Gold store — ranked by the app's like counts.

Source: Dianping 推荐菜 · TOP 1–10

  1. 本物红罐牛肉
    Red-Jar Beef No. 1

    The top order: thin-sliced beef brought in a red jar and grilled at the table. One bite, a lot of juice — the cheapest way to taste the beef.

  2. 霜降厚切和牛
    Thick-Cut Marbled Wagyu ¥498 No. 2

    Cut thick, grilled rare, finished with sea salt and pepper only. The purest expression of the beef on the menu.

  3. 镇店之宝·至尊金牛
    House Treasure — Golden Bull ¥1,988 No. 3

    The showpiece: a full wagyu platter presented on a golden bull. Order it for a celebration, and book it ahead.

  4. 霜降和牛小排
    Marbled Wagyu Short Rib ¥368 No. 4

    The one most tables order — marbling like a print, fat rendering into the rice. 100+ sold in six months.

  5. 纯血·至尊牛小排
    Pure-Blood Wagyu Short Rib ¥698 No. 5

    A step up from the standard rib: more fat, more depth, cooked a little slower. Share it between two.

  6. 和牛之花
    Wagyu Flower ¥198 No. 6

    Thin slices arranged as a rose. Pretty, and genuinely good — the petals cook in seconds.

  7. 烈焰牛排
    Flame Steak ¥238 No. 7

    Thick-cut wagyu steak, seared hard and rested. The order if you want one piece of meat rather than a tasting.

  8. 烈焰和牛炒饭
    Wagyu Fried Rice ¥58 No. 8

    The cheapest thing here and one of the best: rice fried with wagyu trim and egg. 600+ sold in six months.

  9. 特选·M9+霸王横隔膜
    M9+ Wagyu Outside Skirt ¥328 No. 9

    Skirt steak with the deepest beef flavour on the menu. Chewier than the rib, and worth it.

  10. 纯血·至尊眼肉
    Pure-Blood Wagyu Ribeye ¥898 No. 10

    Brought to the table in a box, then torched with a blue flame in front of you. Theatre, but the meat is the point.

Prices from the Dianping listing; the red-jar beef is part of a set. The ¥2,993 six-person pure-blood wagyu banquet is the value route for a group, and the 100-yuan voucher sells for ¥82 (up to four per table).
