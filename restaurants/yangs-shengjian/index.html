Yang's Fried Dumplings (小杨生煎)

朱家角古镇店

Zhujiajiao, Qingpu · Shanghai

CuisineShanghai snack / shengjian · 生煎・上海小吃
Price¥25–40 per person
1No. 1 Shengjian in QingpuDianping 好评榜
4.1Dianping 4.1 / 52,657 reviews — read the bun score, not the room

The Shanghai chain that made soup-filled shengjian a household name — at ¥6 a bun.

小杨生煎 started in 1994 on Wujiang Road with one shop and one idea: a shengjian with more soup than the old style, folded pleat-side down and fried until the base is crisp. It became a chain — one of the few Shanghai snack brands that travelled outside the city — and the bun is now the reference point for the form.

This branch is in Zhujiajiao's old town, open from 06:30 to 22:00 and priced for locals and day-trippers alike: ¥28 a head. You queue at the counter, take a tray, and eat at a plastic table. It is not a restaurant in any ceremonial sense.

The 4.1 overall score is worth reading carefully. The signature bun has 5,593 likes — by far the most-liked item in the shop — while the room, the queue and the service drag the average down. Judge it on the bun.

Why it became famous

What the city, the guides and the queues actually agree on.

  • ◆The bun: pork and jelly filling, crisp base, soup that bursts on the first bite.
  • ◆5,593 likes on the signature shengjian — the most-liked dish in the shop.
  • ◆A 1994 Shanghai original that turned a street snack into a city-wide chain.
  • ◆Absurdly cheap: ¥6 for the classic bun, ¥28 for a full meal.

Top 10 must-try

Dianping 推荐菜, TOP 1–10 for the Zhujiajiao branch — ranked by the app's like counts.

Source: Dianping 推荐菜 · TOP 1–10

  1. 小杨生煎
    Yang's Pork Shengjian ¥6 No. 1

    The one that built the chain: pork and jelly filling, pleat-side down, crisp base, soup that bursts on the first bite.

  2. 老鸭粉丝汤
    Duck and Vermicelli Soup ¥17 Soup

    The classic pairing — duck broth, glass noodles, a little pepper. The 藤椒 version adds Sichuan pepper heat.

  3. 藤椒大虾生煎
    Sichuan-Pepper Prawn Shengjian ¥22 Variant

    The same bun with a whole prawn and a numbing note. Order it alongside the pork, not instead.

  4. 鱼丸汤
    Fish Ball Soup ¥12 Soup

    Springy fish balls in clear broth — the cheap order that makes the meal a meal.

  5. 特色酸辣粉
    House Hot and Sour Noodles ¥14 Noodles

    Sweet potato noodles, vinegar and chilli. The non-bun order for anyone who wants heat.

  6. 荠菜生煎
    Shepherd's Purse Shengjian ¥12 Variant

    The vegetarian option: greens rather than pork, same crisp base. Order it in spring.

  7. 里脊肉
    Pork Tenderloin Skewers ¥6 Side

    Grilled skewers, springy and peppery. The side that turns two buns into a meal.

  8. 特色炸猪排
    House Fried Pork Chop ¥15 Fried

    Thin, crisp, Shanghai-style. Best with the vinegar dip if they have it.

  9. 口水鸡茄香风味肉沫拌面
    Mouth-Watering Chicken Noodles with Minced Pork ¥17.2 Noodles

    Cold chicken, minced pork, tomato-savoury sauce over noodles. The most substantial non-bun plate.

  10. 油豆腐牛肉粉丝汤
    Beef and Fried Tofu Vermicelli Soup ¥17.2 Soup

    Beef slices and fried tofu in broth — the heartiest soup on the list.

Prices from the Dianping listing. The app vouchers are the value: ¥4 for a single bun (from ¥12) and ¥17.4 for the set meal. Cash prices at the counter are higher.
