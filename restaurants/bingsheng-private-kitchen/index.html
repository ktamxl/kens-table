Bingsheng Private Kitchen (炳胜私厨)

中达旗舰店

Zhujiang New Town, Tianhe · Guangzhou

CuisineCantonese, fine dining · 粤菜・私房菜
Price¥498–800 per person
珠Black Pearl — Two Diamonds2026 黑珍珠二钻餐厅
4.8Dianping 4.8 / 52,110 reviews at the time of listing

The Bingsheng group's fine-dining room — two Black Pearl diamonds, and a ¥15 bun at the top of the list.

炳胜私厨 is the formal room in the Bingsheng group — the one that takes the roast meats and dim sum that made 炳胜品味 famous and serves them at a slower pace, in a quieter room, at three times the price. It holds two Black Pearl diamonds as of 2026, the highest award in the group.

The menu is still Cantonese, but the register is different: Doumen roe crab braised with black-hair melon, claypot rice with Yunnan ham and dried ceps, thirty-year Xinhui tangerine peel double-boiled with bird's nest, and the group's signature goose upgraded with truffle and a gravy sealed inside.

The chef's name on the banner is 曹嗣全, and the group's own history is on the wall: founded in Guangzhou in 1996, thirty years ago, starting from a seafood stall under Haizhu Bridge. The private kitchen is what that stall became at the top end.

The detail that keeps it honest: the most-liked dish on the menu is a ¥15 pineapple bun. Expensive restaurants are usually judged on their cheapest item, and this one earns it.

Why it became famous

What the city, the guides and the queues actually agree on.

  • ◆Two Black Pearl diamonds — the group's highest-rated room.
  • ◆Doumen roe crab with black-hair melon, a dish built on the roe rather than the crab meat.
  • ◆The Bingsheng goose with truffle and sealed gravy — the fine-dining version of the signature.
  • ◆A ¥15 pineapple bun that outsells everything on a ¥498 menu.

Top 10 must-try

Dianping 推荐菜, TOP 1–10 for the Zhongda flagship — ranked by the app's like counts.

Source: Dianping 推荐菜 · TOP 1–10

  1. 菠萝酥皮大包
    Pineapple Crisp Bun ¥15 No. 1

    The most-liked item on the list and the cheapest: a sugar-crust top that shatters, soft bread under it. Order it at the start, not the end.

  2. 烧味小三拼
    Small BBQ Trio ¥188 No. 2

    Char siu, roast goose and crispy pork on one plate. The dark char siu is the one to note — slightly bitter at the edges, in the right way.

  3. 炳胜辣虾菇玉带水晶饺
    Bingsheng Chilli Shrimp and Scallop Dumpling No. 3

    A crystal dumpling with shrimp, scallop and a chilli edge. The dim sum that shows the kitchen is not only about roast meats.

  4. 炳胜辣海胆鲜虾水晶饺
    Bingsheng Chilli Sea Urchin and Shrimp Dumpling ¥58 No. 4

    The luxury version — sea urchin folded into the shrimp. Order one basket between two.

  5. 斗门膏蟹焖黑毛节瓜
    Doumen Roe Crab Braised with Black-Hair Melon ¥458 No. 5

    Crab roe melting into the melon until the vegetable tastes of shellfish. The best dish on the list for the money.

  6. 云腿干巴菌啫熊猫米饭
    Yunnan Ham and Cep Rice in a Claypot ¥298 No. 6

    Claypot rice with Yunnan ham and dried ceps — nutty, savoury, with a crust at the bottom. The carbohydrate order of the night.

  7. 泰国香茅玻璃脆皮乳鸽
    Glass-Skin Pigeon with Thai Lemongrass No. 7

    Skin like glass, lemongrass in the marinade rather than on top. The best pigeon in the group.

  8. 新会东甲三十年陈皮炖燕窝
    30-Year Tangerine Peel and Bird's Nest ¥288 No. 8

    Double-boiled with thirty-year Xinhui peel — sweet, faintly bitter, expensive. The dessert that is not really a dessert.

  9. 黑松露灌汤脆皮烧鹅
    Black Truffle Crispy Roast Goose with Gravy ¥188 No. 9

    The group's signature goose, upgraded with truffle and a gravy sealed inside. Order it if you have not had the 品味 version.

  10. 深海拆鱼羹
    Hand-Shredded Deep-Sea Fish Soup No. 10

    Fish picked apart by hand and thickened into a soup with garoupa. Gentle, and the right thing after the crab.

Prices from the Dianping listing; a few items are only available inside the set menus, so no price is shown. Sets: ¥760 three-person lunch, ¥1,291 two-person Black Pearl menu, ¥1,691 three-person chef's menu. The 200-yuan voucher sells for ¥182.
