Lai Lai Xiao Long (莱莱小笼·乔艾)

天津路店

East Nanjing Road · Shanghai

CuisineShanghai xiaolongbao · 小笼・上海点心
Price¥50–80 per person
必MICHELIN Bib Gourmand2024, 2025 and 2026 — three consecutive years

1No. 1 Small Wonton in ShanghaiDianping 王牌菜 · 菜品榜

An alley xiaolongbao shop with three city-chart titles and a MICHELIN Bib Gourmand three years running.

莱莱小笼·乔艾 sits in an alley off Tianjin Road, in the old block behind East Nanjing Road — the kind of address you walk past twice. Inside it is bright, tiled and entirely functional: an open kitchen, a few tables, a queue that starts before service.

You can watch the xiaolong being folded through the glass. That is the whole proposition: hand-folded dumplings, crab roe from the same morning, and a pot of ginger-thread vinegar on every table. The MICHELIN guide has given it a Bib Gourmand for three consecutive years, 2024 through 2026.

The Dianping charts are unusually specific about this shop: No. 1 xiaolongbao in Shanghai, No. 1 small wonton, No. 4 fried pork chop. Those are city-wide rankings, not district ones — which is why the queue exists.

One caveat: the 4.3 overall score reflects a cheap, fast, crowded room, not the food. Judge it on the dumplings.

Top 10 must-try

Dianping 推荐菜, TOP 1–10 for the Tianjin Road shop — ranked by the app's like counts, with the city-chart positions where they exist.

Source: Dianping 推荐菜 · TOP 1–10

  1. 蟹粉鲜肉小笼8只
    Crab Roe and Pork Xiaolong (8) ¥46 No. 1

    No. 1 on Dianping's Shanghai xiaolongbao chart. Thin skin, pork and crab roe, soup that arrives before the filling does.

  2. 姜丝醋
    Ginger-Thread Vinegar ¥1 Condiment

    A yuan's worth of the thing that makes the rest work: sharp, sweet-sour, cuts the crab fat. Order one per person.

  3. 炸猪排
    Fried Pork Chop ¥25 No. 4

    No. 4 on Shanghai's pork-chop chart. Crisp, thin, with Worcestershire-style sauce — the Shanghai way.

  4. 蟹粉面
    Crab Roe Noodles ¥138 Crab

    A whole bowl of crab roe over noodles. The most expensive thing here, and the one to order if you are not counting.

  5. 蟹粉小馄饨10只
    Crab Roe Wontons (10) ¥32 No. 1

    No. 1 small wonton in Shanghai. Skin with a little bite, filling sweet rather than salty.

  6. 鸡鸭血汤
    Chicken and Duck Blood Soup ¥15 Soup

    Clear broth, blood curd, coriander. The old Shanghai accompaniment to a plate of xiaolong.

  7. 纯蟹膏小笼
    Pure Crab Roe Xiaolong ¥70

    The MICHELIN guide's pick: nothing but crab roe inside, best with the ginger vinegar.

  8. 纯鲜肉馅小笼
    Pure Pork Xiaolong ¥28 Classic

    The control order — no crab, just pork and soup. If this one is good, the kitchen is good.

  9. 松茸鲜肉小笼
    Matsutake and Pork Xiaolong ¥33 Seasonal

    Matsutake folded into the pork. Earthy, and the most seasonal thing on the menu.

  10. 老街炸鸡串
    Old-Street Fried Chicken Skewers ¥12 Snack

    Crisp, salty, five-spice. The snack people order while waiting for a table.

Prices from the Dianping listing. The classic pork xiaolong is ¥28 for eight — the cheapest proper meal in this part of town. The crab roe versions are where the money goes.
