Yushui House (渔水之家·土灶柴火饭)

朱家角店

Zhujiajiao, Qingpu · Shanghai

CuisineJiangsu-Zhejiang country cooking · 苏浙菜
Price¥80–120 per person
1No. 1 Su-Zhe Restaurant in QingpuDianping 好评榜

4.9Dianping 4.9 / 514,421 reviews — rare for a tourist-town address

A wood-fired stove, a river at the door, and the best-reviewed country chicken in Qingpu.

渔水之家 sits a few minutes from the main gate of Zhujiajiao, in an old house with a wood-fired stove at the back. The name means something like 'home of fish and water', and the menu follows: river fish, free-range chicken, vegetables from the farms around the water town.

It has been listed on Dianping for thirteen years and holds a 4.9 across 14,421 reviews, which for an address this close to a tourist gate is unusual — the reviews are not all day-trippers. The room has private tables for seven to ten, and the kitchen will take a phone booking.

The 白斩鸡 carries the ranking: there are two versions, 乡下 and regular, both poached then chilled, chopped bone-in and served with soy and ginger. The country bird is the one with flavour in the meat rather than on the skin. Order both if there are four of you — the comparison is the point.

The other reason to come is the rice. 咸肉菜饭 is cooked in an iron pot and scraped up with the crust mixed through; the stove is not decoration.

Top 10 must-try

Dianping 推荐菜, TOP 1–10 for the Zhujiajiao branch — ranked by the app's like counts, with the dish-chart positions where they exist.

Source: Dianping 推荐菜 · TOP 1–10

  1. 乡下白斩鸡
    Country White-Cut Chicken ¥58 No. 3

    No. 3 on Shanghai's white-cut chicken chart. Poached, chilled, chopped bone-in — the bird tastes of itself, not of the soy.

  2. 古镇扎肉
    Old-Town Lotus-Leaf Pork ¥13 Local

    Zhujiajiao's own snack: pork belly wrapped in dried lotus leaf and braised until it collapses. Order two.

  3. 银鱼炒蛋
    Silver Fish Omelette ¥38 River

    Tiny lake fish folded through egg. The dish that most clearly says 'river town'.

  4. 白斩鸡
    White-Cut Chicken ¥58

    The regular version — same technique, younger bird, softer skin. Order both and compare.

  5. 清蒸白水鱼
    Steamed White Water Fish ¥69 River

    Steamed whole with ginger and scallion. Nothing to hide behind, so the fish has to be fresh. It is.

  6. 油爆虾
    Flash-Fried River Shrimp ¥88 Wok

    Shells crisp enough to eat, wok heat all the way through. The best non-chicken order on the list.

  7. 咸肉菜饭
    Salted Pork and Greens Rice ¥38 Rice

    Cooked in the iron pot, crust scraped up and mixed in. The reason to order rice rather than noodles.

  8. 油焖茭白
    Braised Water Bamboo ¥28 Vegetable

    Sweet soy braise, the Shanghai way — dark, glossy, slightly sweet. A vegetable that eats like a main.

  9. 土鸡蛋
    Farm Egg Omelette ¥38 Egg

    Golden egg, green scallion, nothing else. Simple enough that the stove does the work.

  10. 盐水白米虾
    Salted White Rice Shrimp ¥68 Cold

    Small river shrimp poached in salted water, served cold. Sweet, springy, made for beer.

Prices from the Dianping listing. The 100-yuan voucher sells for ¥81, and there is a 10–12 person banquet set at ¥1,520 if you are bringing the family.
