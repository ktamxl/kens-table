MIAN (紅棉)

中環美利酒店

Central · Hong Kong

CuisineModern Chinese · 新派中菜
PriceHK$600–900 per person

A glass pavilion in Central, cooking across the eight regional cuisines.

紅棉 opened in 2021 in the pavilion of The Murray, the former government headquarters building on Cotton Tree Drive. The room is the point as much as the food: a glass box with an outdoor terrace, set back from the traffic, which in Central is a small luxury.

The kitchen is run by 邵德龍 (Shao Tak-lung), a chef who has worked across regional Chinese cooking rather than within a single tradition. The menu is deliberately non-linear — Cantonese roast meats next to Sichuan heat next to Jiangnan sweetness — and the dish that has travelled furthest on social media is the 別不同叉燒, a char siu built to be different from the classic rather than a copy of it.

It is hotel dining, priced accordingly, and the service is polished in the way hotel rooms are. Go for lunch if you want the pavilion light without the full dinner spend.

A pavilion, a chef, and eight cuisines to move between.The room in one line

Why it became famous

What the city, the guides and the queues actually agree on.

  • ◆The setting: a standalone glass pavilion inside The Murray, with a terrace and no street noise.
  • ◆邵德龍's menu deliberately crosses the eight regional cuisines instead of staying Cantonese.
  • ◆別不同叉燒 — the char siu that food bloggers come for.
  • ◆A wine list and service level built for hosting, not for a quick meal.

Top 10 must-try

OpenRice 食評, the hotel menu and the weekend dim-sum list — the dishes this room is actually known for.

Source: OpenRice 食評 + hotel menu

  1. 紅棉蔥油脆皮雞
    Red Cotton Spring-Onion Crispy Chicken

    The hotel's headline dish: crisp skin, spring-onion oil poured over at the pass. Half a bird is enough for three.

  2. 別不同叉燒
    "Different" Char Siu

    The house's signature roast — cut thick, glazed dark, deliberately not the soft, sweet version.

  3. 有機番茄焗日本黑豚豬扒飯
    Baked Kagoshima Pork Chop Rice with Organic Tomato

    A Hong Kong classic rebuilt with better pork. Comfort food at hotel prices, and worth it once.

  4. 魚湯鮮腐竹浸時蔬
    Seasonal Greens with Fresh Bean Curd Skin in Fish Soup

    The soup is cooked down from fish frames; the vegetables are there to carry it.

  5. 翡翠玉忱（雞濃湯涼瓜絲）
    Jade Cushion — Bitter Melon with Chicken Consommé

    Bitter melon cut into ribbons and poached in a concentrated chicken broth. The dish that shows the kitchen's restraint.

  6. 齊菜河蝦餃
    Shepherd's Purse and River Shrimp Dumpling

    A dim sum of the season — the greens cut the shrimp's sweetness.

  7. 沸騰小籠包
    "Boiling" Xiao Long Bao

    Served hot enough that the soup inside is still moving. Eat it in one bite, spoon underneath.

  8. 鮮回香蟹肉餃
    Crab Meat Dumpling

    Crab rather than pork, finished with a little aged vinegar on the side.

  9. 陳年青缸醉花螺
    Drunken Sea Snails in Aged Rice Wine

    Cold, wine-heavy, served as a starter. The most old-Shanghai thing on a Cantonese menu.

  10. 六款點心拼盤
    Six-Piece Dim Sum Platter

    The weekend brunch format: six dim sum chosen by the kitchen, including the dumplings above.
