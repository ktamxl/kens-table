Bingsheng Pinwei (炳胜品味)

珠江新城旗舰店

Zhujiang New Town, Tianhe · Guangzhou

CuisineCantonese · 粤菜
Price¥150–300 per person
米MICHELIN Guide SelectedNine consecutive years, 2018–2026
必Dianping Must-Eat ListMultiple years running
五National Five-Diamond Restaurant国家五钻级酒家
钻Trip.com Diamond RestaurantBingsheng group, 2025

The 86-square-metre seafood stall that became Guangzhou's Cantonese benchmark.

On 8 August 1996, a seafood stall opened under Haizhu Bridge with 86 square metres, a handful of tables and a single idea: serve the Cantonese food Guangzhou people actually grew up eating, at a standard worth crossing town for. The founders were 曹嗣标 and his brother-in-law 卢润炳 — one ran the floor, the other stood in the kitchen.

The dish that built the reputation was 冰盘鱼生, Shunde-style raw fish laid over ice. It was not a banquet showpiece; it was a discipline. The fish had to be alive that morning, the knife work had to be exact, and the ice had to arrive at the table still dry and cold. Word spread through Haizhu, then across the river.

In 2000 the kitchen did something that sounded like a mistake: it made char siu crisp. Cantonese cooks had spent generations chasing soft, yielding 叉烧, and 炳胜 deliberately went the other way — a thin, glassy crust that shatters before the meat gives way. 秘制脆皮叉烧 became the dish that other restaurants in Guangzhou had to answer.

Thirty years later the group runs a matrix of brands — 炳胜品味 for the everyday table, 炳胜公馆 and 炳胜私厨 for the formal one, 小炳胜 for a younger crowd, 禅意茶素 for vegetarian — with a dedicated recipe development centre and, since late 2018, a foothold in Shenzhen. The flagship on Xiancun Road, four floors opposite the Guangdong Museum, is where the whole thing is easiest to read in a single meal.

炳承信念，旗开得胜。The group's own four-character motto

Why it became famous

What the city, the guides and the queues actually agree on.

  • ◆It is the birthplace of the crisp-school 叉烧 — the 2000 innovation that changed how Guangzhou talks about roast pork.
  • ◆Its 烧味 programme is treated as a syllabus: 灌汤烧鹅, 黑叉烧, 豉油皇鹅肠. Locals call the trio textbook-grade.
  • ◆The 冰盘鱼生 heritage is still on the menu — a Shunde technique most city restaurants have quietly dropped.
  • ◆Nine straight years in the MICHELIN Guide (Selected, 2018–2026) plus repeated 大众点评必吃榜 listings made it a default recommendation for visitors who want one reliable Cantonese meal.
  • ◆The room looks over the Pearl River towards Canton Tower — the view does a lot of the hosting for you.

Top 10 must-try

Ranked from Dianping's 推荐菜 list for the Zhujiang New Town branch — the ten most-recommended dishes, in order.

Source: Dianping 推荐菜 · only the top ten are listed

  1. 秘制脆皮灌汤烧鹅
    Secret-Recipe Crispy Roast Goose with Gravy

    The bird is roasted with a seasoned broth sealed inside, so the first cut releases gravy while the skin stays glass-crisp. This is the plate to judge the kitchen by.

  2. 秘制脆皮叉烧
    Secret-Recipe Crispy Char Siu The original

    The 2000 invention: a caramelised, brittle crust over fatty pork collar. Order it in the two-way platter with the black version to taste the difference side by side.

  3. 秘制黑叉烧
    Secret-Recipe "Black" Char Siu

    Darker, stickier, heavier on aged soy and molasses. Cut thicker than the crisp version, so the inside stays juicy rather than candy-like.

  4. 豉油皇鹅肠
    Soy-Sauce Goose Intestines Local favourite

    Wok-tossed in a sweet-savoury master sauce until the edges catch. Texture is the whole point — springy, never rubbery. With the roast goose and black char siu it forms the “iron triangle” locals order by default.

  5. 原始水晶三味豆腐
    Crystal Three-Flavour Tofu Regulars' order

    Silken tofu set in-house, served three ways in one bowl. A quiet dish that regulars order every visit — useful as a pause between the roast courses.

  6. 章红鱼生
    Amberjack Yüsheng, Shunde Style Heritage

    The dish the original stall was built on: amberjack sliced thin, laid over ice with ginger, scallion, peanuts and oil. Eat it first, before anything fried.

  7. 炳胜烧味三拼
    Bingsheng BBQ Trio Best first order

    Crisp char siu, black char siu and roast goose on one plate. If it is your first visit and the table is small, start here rather than choosing one.

  8. 招牌菠萝包
    Signature Pineapple Bun Dianping ranked

    Ranked around No. 2 for pineapple buns in Guangzhou on Dianping. Order it when you sit down — the kitchen bakes in batches and it is the one item that regularly runs out.

  9. 招积冰川茄
    "Glacier" Chilled Aubergine

    Aubergine fried until the shell is glassy, then dressed in a tangy chilled sauce. Sweet, sour and cold — the dish that resets your palate halfway through a long lunch.

  10. 腊味煲仔饭
    Claypot Rice with Cured Sausage and Preserved Meat Rice

    The closer. Ask for the crisp rice at the bottom and let it sit two minutes before mixing; the lap mei fat needs that time to render into the grains.

From the notebooks

Paraphrased from public Dianping and Xiaohongshu posts — the details the guides do not print.

  • The pineapple bun is the size of a small melon, arrives hot, and hides a pineapple-jam centre. Regulars order it the moment they sit down, because it is the one item that sells out.
  • Black char siu is the richer of the two — more than one diner calls it too fatty to finish alone. Share it, or ask the kitchen for a leaner cut.
  • Weekend lunch queues can run an hour, and the room is built for groups rather than for two. Book, or come early and treat the wait as part of it.
