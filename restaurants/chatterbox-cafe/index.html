Chatterbox Café (文華海南雞飯)

尖沙咀 K11 MUSEA

Tsim Sha Tsui · Hong Kong

CuisineSingaporean, Hainanese · 東南亞菜・海南雞
PriceHK$200–300 per person
5No. 5 Southeast Asian in Tsim Sha TsuiDianping 尖沙咀東南亞菜熱門榜
4.6Dianping 4.6 / 5932 reviews at the time of listing

The chicken rice that started in a Singapore hotel lobby in 1971 — now in a Kowloon mall basement.

Chatterbox opened in 1971 in the Mandarin Singapore, as the hotel's coffee house, and its Hainanese chicken rice became the thing people queued for in a five-star lobby. That is the whole reason this outpost exists: a hotel dish that outgrew the hotel.

The Hong Kong branch sits in the basement of K11 MUSEA, on the Tsim Sha Tsui waterfront — a food-hall setting rather than a dining room, with the same recipe lineage on the menu. It holds 4.6 across 932 reviews, with environment scoring 4.8, and ranks fifth in Tsim Sha Tsui for Southeast Asian cooking.

The order writes itself. The HK$138 chicken rice is the entry point and the most-liked dish by a wide margin; the HK$362 whole bird is for a table of four. Around it: salted egg yolk wings at HK$48, a chicken laksa with real chilli, and a Singapore fried kway teow with the wok hei you would hope for in a hawker centre.

It is a mall restaurant, and it feels like one — but the chicken is the genuine article: poached, chilled, served at room temperature with the three sauces, and rice cooked in the fat. At HK$200–300 a head it is the cheapest way into a name that has been on a hotel menu for fifty-five years.

雞肉滑嫩到入口即化 — the chicken is so silky it dissolves on contact.Dianping reviewers

Why it became famous

What the city, the guides and the queues actually agree on.

  • ◆Hainanese chicken rice from the Mandarin Singapore's Chatterbox, open since 1971.

  • ◆Inside K11 MUSEA, two minutes from East Tsim Sha Tsui station.

Top 8 must-try

Dianping 推薦菜, TOP 1–8 as listed for the K11 MUSEA shop — ranked by the app's like counts.

Source: Dianping 推薦菜 · TOP 1–8

  1. 文華海南雞飯
    Hainanese Chicken Rice HK$138 No. 1

    The dish the shop is known by — 266 likes, more than anything else on the list. Poached chicken at room temperature, rice cooked in the fat, three sauces on the side.

  2. 文華海南雞
    Hainanese Chicken, whole HK$362 No. 2

    The same bird at full size, chopped for the table. Order this when there are four of you and the rice on the side.

  3. 鹹蛋黃雞翼
    Salted Egg Yolk Chicken Wings HK$48 No. 3

    Crisp wings tossed in salted egg yolk until the coating turns grainy and gold. The best value on the menu.

  4. 雞肉喇沙
    Chicken Laksa HK$136 No. 4

    Coconut gravy with a proper chilli edge, chicken and noodles. The reviewers' line: the chicken is silky and the soup is the point.

  5. 海鮮辣沙湯麵
    Seafood Laksa Noodle Soup HK$178 No. 5

    The seafood version — prawns, fish cake, cockles. Richer and thicker than the chicken laksa; split it if you are ordering the rice as well.

  6. 星洲海鮮炒貴刁
    Singapore Seafood Fried Kway Teow HK$128 No. 6

    Wok hei you can smell across the table. Flat rice noodles, prawns, dark soy — the Singapore hawker classic done in a hotel kitchen.

  7. 斑斕奶茶
    Pandan Milk Tea HK$52 No. 7

    Pandan in the tea, not just the colour. Order it iced with the laksa.

  8. 黑醬油炒鮮蝦蘿蔔糕
    Prawn and Radish Cake in Dark Soy HK$96 No. 8

    Turnip cake cubed and fried hard, then tossed with prawns in dark soy. The dish that surprises people who thought they knew radish cake.

Prices in Hong Kong dollars as listed; the Dianping yuan conversions are roughly 0.85 of the HK price. The HK$362 whole chicken feeds three to four with rice and a vegetable.
