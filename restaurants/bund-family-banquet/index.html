Bund Family Banquet (外滩家宴·上海本帮菜)

罗斯福公馆店

the Bund / East Nanjing Road · Shanghai

CuisineShanghainese (benbang) · 本帮菜
Price¥150–250 per person
1No. 1 Jiangzhe Restaurant, East Nanjing RoadDianping 热门榜 — popularity
4.8Dianping 4.8 / 51,747 reviews at the time of listing

Benbang cooking on the Bund, in a room that has been photographed from every angle.

外滩家宴 occupies the ground floor of the Roosevelt Mansion at 27 Zhongshan East 1st Road — one of the few Bund addresses where the view, the room and the bill are all in the same sentence. Dark wood, white cloths, and a menu that is unapologetically benbang: sweet, dark, slow.

It has become part of the 1990s-Shanghai trail partly because it appears in 繁花 (Blossoms), the television adaptation of Jin Yucheng's novel. The listing advertises the connection, and the room leans into it — this is a place people book for the atmosphere as much as the cooking.

The kitchen's strength is crab roe in four forms: noodles, xiao long bao, spring rolls, and a plain bowl of roe over noodles. If you order one thing, make it one of those. The squirrel fish is the most-ordered dish in the house and the one the dining room is built around.

Benbang cooking with a Bund view — the crab roe does the heavy lifting.The house in one line

Why it became famous

What the city, the guides and the queues actually agree on.

  • ◆No. 1 Jiangzhe restaurant in the East Nanjing Road cluster on Dianping's popularity chart.
  • ◆A 繁花 filming location, on the Bund — the view from the room is the Lujiazui skyline.
  • ◆Crab roe in four forms, from ¥26 spring rolls to ¥138 noodles.
  • ◆4.8 across 1,747 reviews, which for a Bund tourist address is unusually high.

Top 10 must-try

Dianping 推荐菜, TOP 1–10 for the Roosevelt Mansion branch — the ranking is by the app's like counts.

Source: Dianping 推荐菜 · TOP 1–10

  1. 海派松鼠鱼
    Shanghai-Style Squirrel Fish ¥158 No. 1

    The top order by a distance. Sweet-and-sour, scored and fried into shape — the dish the room is photographed around.

  2. 海派红烧肉
    Shanghai-Style Red-Braised Pork Belly ¥188

    Dark, sweet, collapsing. Order rice; the sauce is the point as much as the pork.

  3. 蟹黄金捞面
    Crab Roe Noodles, Tossed ¥138 Crab

    Every strand coated in golden crab roe. The dish most tables order twice.

  4. 秘制花雕熟醉虾
    Huadiao-Drunk Prawns ¥58 Cold

    Cooked then marinated in Huadiao wine — sweet, mellow, not sharp. Served cold.

  5. 蟹黄小笼包
    Crab Roe Xiao Long Bao ¥68 Dim sum

    Thin skin, soup inside, crab roe through the filling. Eat it in one bite.

  6. 松茸羊肚菌鸡汤
    Matsutake and Morel Chicken Soup ¥38 Soup

    The cheap order that anchors the table: clear chicken broth with dried mushrooms.

  7. 蟹黄面
    Crab Roe Noodles, Plain ¥138 Crab

    The same crab roe without the toss — a full bowl of it, nothing else in the way.

  8. 蟹黄春卷
    Crab Roe Spring Rolls ¥26 Fried

    Crisp outside, soft crab inside. The cheapest way to taste the crab roe.

  9. 秘制蛋汁大排
    Egg-Batter Pork Chop ¥58

    Pork chop in an egg batter, fried gold. A Shanghai home dish done properly.

  10. 法式黑椒牛肉粒
    Black Pepper Beef Cubes ¥158 Theatre

    Flambeed at the table, the one French-inflected dish on a benbang menu.

Prices from the Dianping listing. Two set menus do most of the work: ¥390 for two (百年公馆 主厨推荐) and ¥590 for four (上海家宴). The 100-yuan voucher sells for ¥89.9 in the app.
