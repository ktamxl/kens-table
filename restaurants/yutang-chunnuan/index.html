Yutang Chunnuan (玉堂春暖)

白天鹅宾馆

Shamian Island, Liwan · Guangzhou

CuisineCantonese · 粤菜
Price¥400–600 per person
珠Black Pearl — Two Diamonds2026 黑珍珠二钻餐厅

The White Swan's Cantonese dining room — two Black Pearl diamonds, and the best char siu bun in Guangzhou.

The White Swan opened on Shamian Island in 1983 as one of China's first joint-venture five-star hotels, built on the river with a garden running through the lobby. 玉堂春暖 is its Cantonese dining room, on the third floor, overlooking the water and the indoor waterfall.

It has been on Dianping for twenty-two years and holds two Black Pearl diamonds as of 2026 — but the reviews are not tourist reviews: 5,289 are local. That is the number that matters for a hotel restaurant on an island everyone visits once.

The menu is the Guangzhou canon, done at a level the city's hotel restaurants rarely reach: Iberico char siu ranked second in Guangzhou, a char siu bun ranked first, white-cut sunflower chicken, and a sachima that has been saved 607 times — more than any other dish here.

Environment scores 4.9 and service 4.9. It is expensive by local standards and still full; book for dinner and ask for a table by the window.

Top 10 must-try

Dianping 推荐菜, TOP 1–10 for the White Swan Hotel — ranked by the app's like counts, with dish-chart positions where they exist.

Source: Dianping 推荐菜 · TOP 1–10

  1. 榄仁萨其马
    Sachima with Olive Kernels ¥68 No. 1

    The most-saved dish in the restaurant — 607 saves. Loose, not sticky, with olive kernels through it. The best version of a humble pastry in the city.

  2. 伊比利亚黑毛猪叉烧
    Iberico Pork Char Siu ¥268 No. 2

    No. 2 char siu in Guangzhou. Iberico pork instead of local — nuttier fat, deeper char. Order it with rice, not as a starter.

  3. 白切葵花鸡
    White-Cut Sunflower Chicken ¥368 No. 3

    Half a bird, poached and chilled, skin yellow and translucent. The chicken equivalent of a good consommé: nothing to hide behind.

  4. 羊城鲜虾饺
    Canton Shrimp Dumpling ¥68 No. 4

    Wrapper thin enough to read through, whole shrimp, bamboo shoot. The benchmark against which every other dim sum list should be judged.

  5. 酥皮叉烧包
    Crisp-Topped Char Siu Bun ¥78 No. 5

    No. 1 char siu bun in Guangzhou. The top cracks and shatters; the filling is savoury rather than sweet. Order two.

  6. 鲍鱼天鹅酥
    Abalone Swan Pastry ¥96 No. 6

    A swan-shaped pastry filled with abalone. The most photographed thing on the menu, and it tastes better than it needs to.

  7. 香茅乳鸽
    Lemongrass Pigeon No. 7

    No. 5 pigeon in Guangzhou. Lemongrass in the marinade, skin roasted hard. The price shown in the app is a promotional one — ask.

  8. 化皮蛋挞
    Flaky Egg Tart ¥65 No. 8

    The pastry is the point: layer on layer, custard just set. Eat it within a minute of it reaching the table.

  9. 蜜汁蒸凤爪
    Steamed Chicken Feet in Honey Sauce ¥65 No. 9

    Soft enough to fall off the bone, sweetness restrained. The dim sum that separates a good kitchen from a great one.

  10. 白卤水鹅掌翼
    Poached Goose Webs and Wings ¥148 No. 10

    Poached in white master stock rather than soy — cleaner, more delicate. The dish to order with a glass of something cold.

Prices from the Dianping listing. The Black Pearl tasting menus start at ¥260 (Iberico char siu set) and run to the ¥1,128 set for two with wine. The pigeon price shown in the app is promotional — confirm at the table.
