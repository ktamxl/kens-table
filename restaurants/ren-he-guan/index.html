Ren He Guan (人和馆)

徐汇店

Xuhui · Shanghai

CuisineShanghainese (benbang) · 本幫菜
Price¥250–400 per person
★MICHELIN One StarSix consecutive years

Six straight years of a MICHELIN star, with 1930s songs in the dining room.

人和馆 builds its room out of a very specific memory: 1930s Shanghai, when a restaurant was also a dance floor and a stage. The dining room is fitted in dark wood and period glass, and a small band works through old Shanghai standards while the tables eat.

The kitchen does not modernise. Benbang cooking is defined by 浓油赤酱 — heavy oil, heavy soy, heavy sugar — and 人和馆 applies it without apology: braised pork, oil-exploded shrimp, eel shredded and stir-fried until it caramelises at the edges.

The MICHELIN Guide has given it one star for six consecutive years, which in Shanghai is a statement about consistency rather than novelty. Book ahead for dinner, and expect the music to be loud enough that you will lean across the table.

Why it became famous

What the city, the guides and the queues actually agree on.

  • ◆Six consecutive MICHELIN stars — consistency rather than trend-chasing.
  • ◆Live 1930s Shanghai songs during service; the room is designed as theatre.
  • ◆A textbook 浓油赤酱 benbang menu: 红烧肉, 油爆虾, 鳝丝, 划水.
  • ◆One of the few places where a visitor can hear the city's old popular music while eating its old food.

Top 10 must-try

The Xuhui menu's most-ordered dishes — benbang standards, heavy on soy and sugar.

Source: Dianping 推荐菜 · top ten only

  1. 金牌红烧肉
    Signature Red-Braised Pork Belly

    Sweet, dark and collapsing. Order rice with it, not noodles.

  2. 油爆虾
    Flash-Fried River Shrimp

    Eat the shells. The sugar in the glaze should be just short of burnt.

  3. 本帮熏鱼
    Benbang Smoked Fish

    Cold, sticky, sweet-savoury. The first thing to order.

  4. 老上海酱爆猪肝
    Old-Shanghai Pork Liver in Bean Sauce

    Fast wok, soft liver, a lot of sauce. Order it to share.

  5. 蟹粉捞饭
    Crab Roe over Rice

    Served by the small or large portion, dine-in only. The most expensive comfort food on the menu.

  6. 松鼠桂鱼
    Squirrel Mandarin Fish

    Sweet-and-sour, scored and fried into shape. A banquet dish, priced like one.

  7. 草头圈子
    Braised Pork Intestine with Clover

    The dish that separates locals from visitors. Cleaned properly, it has no off note.

  8. 酱鸭
    Soy-Braised Duck

    Cold, dark, sliced thin. A benbang starter.

  9. 响油鳝丝
    Sizzling Shredded Eel

    Hot oil at the table, then stir before the eel cools.

  10. 酒酿圆子
    Fermented Rice Wine with Glutinous Balls

    The dessert. Warm, faintly alcoholic, not too sweet.
