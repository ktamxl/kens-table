Dian Dou De (点都德)

深圳分店

Multiple districts · Shenzhen

CuisineCantonese teahouse / dim sum · 粵式茶樓・點心
Price¥80–130 per person
老Guangzhou Old Brand广州老字号
非Intangible Heritage Unit非物质文化遗产保育单位

A 1933 Guangzhou teahouse that still makes dim sum by hand.

点都德 traces itself to 1933, when 沈绍清 returned from abroad, married into a Longgui family and took over their teahouse, renaming it 德香楼. It closed during the Cultural Revolution and was revived in stages — a restaurant here, a private kitchen there — before the first 点都德大茶楼 opened in 2013.

The name is Cantonese slang: 点都德, roughly "whatever you order, it's fine" — the phrase a host uses to wave away a guest's hesitation. The brand leans hard into that idea, with trolleys, loud rooms and a menu of more than fifty dim sum.

It holds 广州老字号 status and is listed as a heritage conservation unit for Cantonese teahouse culture. The Shenzhen branches are the practical option for anyone staying on this side of the border: same kitchen system, same hand-folded dumplings, shorter queue.

Why it became famous

What the city, the guides and the queues actually agree on.

  • ◆Dim sum is still made by hand in-house rather than bought in frozen — increasingly rare at this price.
  • ◆A 1933 lineage, officially recognised as a Guangzhou old brand.
  • ◆Trolley service and a menu long enough to eat across three visits.
  • ◆Shenzhen branches make it the default recommendation for visitors who want yum cha without crossing to Hong Kong.

Top 10 must-try

Dianping 推荐菜 and the house's three classics: shrimp rice roll, shrimp dumpling, satay tripe.

Source: Dianping 推荐菜 · top ten only

  1. 金沙鲜虾红米肠
    Crispy Shrimp Rice Roll

    The house's most-ordered item: red rice wrapper, crisp middle, whole shrimp.

  2. 金牌虾饺皇
    Signature Shrimp Dumpling

    Translucent wrapper, whole shrimp, no filler. The benchmark.

  3. 沙爹金钱肚
    Satay Beef Tripe

    Braised soft, finished in a thick satay sauce. Order two baskets.

  4. 百合酱蒸凤爪
    Chicken Feet in Lily-Bulb Sauce

    Sticky, savoury, faintly sweet. The other two-basket order.

  5. 蜜汁叉烧包
    Honey Char Siu Bao

    Split-top, soft, proper sweet-savoury filling.

  6. 干蒸烧卖
    Pork and Shrimp Siu Mai

    The plain version, and a reliable test of the steamer.

  7. 艇仔粥
    Sampan Congee

    Rice congee with peanuts, squid and pork skin — the teahouse staple.

  8. 咸煎饼
    Salty Fried Cake

    Chewy, faintly fermented, sesame-coated. Order it to share.

  9. 鲜虾腐皮卷
    Shrimp Bean-Curd Roll

    Fried to order. Do not let it sit.

  10. 香煎萝卜糕
    Pan-Fried Turnip Cake

    Crisp edges, peppery centre.
