Siji Minfu Roast Duck (四季民福烤鸭店)

静安大悦城店

Suzhou Creek / Jing'an · Shanghai

CuisineBeijing roast duck · 北京菜・烤鸭
Price¥180–260 per person
1No. 1 Beijing Restaurant in ShanghaiDianping 销量榜 — by sales
4.8Dianping 4.8 / 517,828 reviews at the time of listing

Beijing duck in Shanghai, carved at the table — and the city's best-selling Beijing restaurant.

四季民福 built its name in Beijing on roast duck at a price that does not require an occasion, and on a menu that reaches past duck into the old Beijing repertoire — 爆肚, 贝勒烤肉, 炸酱面, 驴打滚. This is the Shanghai room, on the fifth floor of Jing'an Joy City, three years old and already the city's best-selling Beijing restaurant.

The duck arrives whole, is carved beside the table, and is served in the correct order: skin first, dipped in sugar, then the meat with pancakes, scallion and sweet bean sauce. The skin is the test — if it is not glassy, nothing else matters.

The rest of the menu is there to stop the duck from being too much: cold pea shoots dressed sharply, a clear meatball broth, noodles with fried bean sauce. Order across the menu rather than ordering two ducks.

Why it became famous

What the city, the guides and the queues actually agree on.

  • ◆No. 1 Beijing restaurant in Shanghai by sales on Dianping, with 17,828 reviews at 4.8.
  • ◆Duck carved at the table, skin served first with sugar — the Beijing protocol, done properly.

  • ◆A chain, but a disciplined one: the same duck, the same carving, in a shopping-centre room.

Top 10 must-try

Dianping 推荐菜, TOP 1–10 for the Jing'an Joy City branch — ranked by the app's like counts.

Source: Dianping 推荐菜 · TOP 1–10

  1. 酥香嫩烤鸭
    Crisp, Fragrant, Tender Roast Duck ¥259 No. 1

    The whole point. Carved at the table, skin first, with pancakes, scallion and sweet bean sauce. Order a half duck for two, a whole for four.

  2. 巧拌豆苗
    Tossed Pea Shoots ¥45 Cold

    The dish that made the top two: raw-tasting shoots dressed sharp and cold. It cuts the duck fat better than anything else on the table.

  3. 贝勒烤肉
    Beile Grilled Lamb ¥98 Grill

    An old Beijing dish — lamb off the grill, cumin-heavy, served sizzling. Order it if there are four of you.

  4. 蜜汁酥皮虾
    Honey-Glazed Crisp Prawns ¥98 Fried

    Prawns fried in shell then glazed. Sweet, crisp, gone in three minutes.

  5. 炸酱面
    Zhajiang Noodles ¥26 Noodles

    Noodles with fried bean sauce and a plate of raw vegetables. The cheapest proper order on the menu, and the one to judge the kitchen by.

  6. 京味爆肚
    Beijing-Style Tripe ¥98 Offal

    Blanched to the second — crisp, not chewy — with sesame dipping sauce. The most Beijing thing on the list.

  7. 京城酥肉锅
    Capital Crisp Pork Hot Pot ¥79 Stew

    Fried pork in a broth that softens the crust and soaks it. Winter food, and the thing to order with the duck.

  8. 老北京小吃拼盘
    Old Beijing Snack Platter ¥79 Sweets

    Five small pastries on one plate — the cheapest way to try the sweets without committing to a whole portion of each.

  9. 清炖手工狮子头
    Clear-Braised Lion's Head Meatball ¥45 Soup

    Small, delicate, in a light broth. The quiet dish between the duck and the fried courses.

  10. 驴打滚
    Ludagun (Glutinous Roll) ¥25 Dessert

    Glutinous rice rolled in soybean flour — soft, not too sweet. The dessert to finish on.

Prices from the Dianping listing. The set menus are the value: ¥407 for the duck set for two (入选十年·民福甄选) and ¥833 for four. The 100-yuan voucher sells for ¥90.
