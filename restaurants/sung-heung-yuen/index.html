Sung Heung Yuen (Kin Kee) (新香園（堅記）)

深水埗

Sham Shui Po · Hong Kong

CuisineCha chaan teng · 茶餐廳
PriceHK$40–70 per person
Show this to the waiter畀侍應睇
蛋牛治，唔該。
Beef and egg sandwich, please.

蛋牛治 is the shorthand: 蛋 for egg, 牛 for beef, 治 for 三文治. At 新香園 it is the only thing you need to say.

One sandwich, done since 1968: beef and egg.

新香園 opened on Kweilin Street in 1968 and has been running ever since, now under the second generation of the family that founded it. It is a cha chaan teng in the old sense: formica tables, a menu on the wall, and a kitchen that has been making the same sandwich for over fifty years.

The 蛋牛治 is the reason people cross the district for it. Hand-chopped beef, not mince, folded into a fresh omelette and pressed between thick white bread — the egg still soft, the beef still juicy, the whole thing held together by the egg rather than by sauce. It is a two-hand sandwich and it costs less than a coffee in Central.

The shop is open around the clock, which in Sham Shui Po is its own kind of public service. Order at the counter, take a number, and do not expect to linger at peak hours.

One sandwich, fifty years, no need to look at the menu.The house order

Why it became famous

What the city, the guides and the queues actually agree on.

  • ◆蛋牛治 — hand-chopped beef and egg sandwich, the house specialty since 1968.
  • ◆Open 24 hours, which makes it a late-night and early-morning institution.
  • ◆Silk-stocking milk tea with a heavy tea base, brewed the old way.
  • ◆Second-generation family ownership — the recipe has not changed.

Top 10 must-try

A 1968 cha chaan teng menu — one sandwich carries the reputation, the rest is the standard repertoire.

Source: OpenRice 必食 + house menu

  1. 蛋牛治
    Beef and Egg Sandwich

    The order. Ask for 烘底 if you want the bread toasted — it costs extra and changes the texture completely.

  2. 西多士
    French Toast

    The other thing people order. Share it.

  3. 絲襪奶茶
    Silk-Stocking Milk Tea

    Strong, dense, served hot. The correct drink with the sandwich.

  4. 菠蘿油
    Pineapple Bun with Butter

    Ordered hot, butter still melting.

  5. 餐蛋麵
    Luncheon Meat and Egg Noodles

    The all-day fallback, and the reason the shop never closes.

  6. 沙爹牛肉麵
    Satay Beef Noodles

    Thick sauce, soft noodles. A 3am order.

  7. 凍檸茶
    Iced Lemon Tea

    Ask for 少甜; the default is heavy on the syrup.

  8. 火腿通粉
    Ham and Macaroni Soup

    Clear broth, elbow macaroni. The savoury anchor of a set.

  9. 奶油多
    Condensed Milk Toast

    Warm toast, condensed milk. Simpler than the French toast and, for many, better.

  10. 紅豆冰
    Red Bean Ice

    Condensed milk and crushed ice. The summer drink.
