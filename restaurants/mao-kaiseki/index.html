Mao Kaiseki (茂·会席料理·OMAKASE·烧鸟·天妇罗)

广粤天地店

Xingsheng Road / Racecourse, Tianhe · Guangzhou

CuisineJapanese kaiseki / omakase · 日本料理・会席
Price¥500–860 per person
2No. 2 Japanese Restaurant for Taste, Xingsheng RoadDianping 口味榜
4.8Dianping 4.8 / 5126 reviews — a small, young room

A small omakase room in Guangyue Tiandi — 126 reviews, second in the district for taste.

茂 is a small Japanese room in Guangyue Tiandi, behind the north gate near the Tesla showroom — the kind of address that exists for people who already know it. Eleven-thirty to two, five-thirty to eleven, private rooms, phone booking only.

It runs omakase rather than an à la carte menu: ¥530 for the 甄选 set, ¥860 for the 极上, and lunch sets at ¥240 (yakitori and sushi) and ¥282 (twelve pieces of sushi). The kitchen also does yakitori and tempura, which is why the name lists all three.

The reviews are few — 126 — so treat the 4.8 as promising rather than proven. What is proven is the taste ranking: No. 2 in the Xingsheng Road Japanese chart, which is a district with a lot of Japanese restaurants.

Order the ¥530 set and add the ¥20 kinmedai sushi and the ¥38 sesame ice cream. That is the useful version of this restaurant.

Top 10 must-try

Dianping 推荐菜, TOP 1–10 for the Guangyue Tiandi branch — ranked by the app's like counts.

Source: Dianping 推荐菜 · TOP 1–10

  1. 蓝鳍金枪鱼配法国鹅肝
    Bluefin Tuna with French Foie Gras No. 1

    The top order, served inside the ¥530 omakase. Tuna and foie gras on the same bite — rich against rich, cut by a little acid.

  2. 海胆
    Sea Urchin No. 2

    Fresh urchin, no garnish beyond salt. Comes in the set; the sweetness is the point.

  3. 日本星鳗配泰国香菜
    Japanese Anago with Thai Coriander No. 3

    Conger eel brushed with sweet soy, coriander on top. The coriander is the surprise — it lifts the glaze.

  4. 海胆手卷
    Sea Urchin Hand Roll No. 4

    Nori, warm rice, urchin. Eat it the second it reaches you — the nori softens fast.

  5. 黑松露和牛
    Black Truffle Wagyu No. 5

    From the ¥860 极上 set: seared wagyu with shaved truffle. The most expensive thing the kitchen serves.

  6. 自家制甜品
    House-Made Dessert ¥38 No. 6

    Sesame ice cream, churned in-house — nutty, not too sweet. The only dessert on the list you can order alone.

  7. 金鲷
    Kinmedai (Golden Snapper) Sushi ¥20 No. 7

    One piece, seared skin side, served warm. The cheapest single item and the best value.

  8. 本鲔寿司
    Hon-Maguro Sushi ¥38 No. 8

    Bluefin akami, aged briefly, rice packed loose. The 12-course lunch is built around this.

  9. 海螺刺身
    Conch Sashimi No. 9

    Sliced thin, crunchy, served cold. A texture course between the fatty fish.

  10. 黑松露茶碗蒸
    Black Truffle Chawanmushi ¥48 No. 10

    Steamed egg with truffle oil — soft, savoury, the dish to order if you want something warm between sashimi.

Most of the top ten are courses inside the omakase, so they carry no separate price. The ¥240 lunch set (yakitori and sushi) and ¥282 twelve-piece sushi set are the value entries; the 100-yuan voucher sells for ¥82.
