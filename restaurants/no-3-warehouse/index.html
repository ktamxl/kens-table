No. 3 Warehouse (3号仓库·创意中国菜)

新世界城店

People's Square / Nanjing Road · Shanghai

CuisineCreative Chinese · 创意中国菜
Price¥150–250 per person
1No. 1 Check-In Spot — Huangpu Creative DiningDianping 打卡人气榜 — check-in popularity
4.7Dianping 4.7 / 532,842 reviews at the time of listing

A ten-branch chain that turned creative Chinese into theatre — dry ice, flame, and a glass-skinned squab.

3号仓库 is a design-led chain of roughly ten branches that treats a meal as a set piece: mirrored ceilings, hanging plants, dry ice and flames at the table. This branch sits on the fourth floor of New World City, about 140 metres from People's Square station — a location built for foot traffic and photographs.

The menu is billed as 创意中国菜, creative Chinese. In practice that means Cantonese roast technique (the glass-skinned squab), chilli-heavy ribs, and a lot of tableside theatre: the abalone rice arrives with a pour, the tea-smoked ribs are lit in front of you, the beef comes out under dry ice.

One honest caveat. This is a photogenic restaurant first and a food destination second; reviews are split, and the room and presentation do a lot of the work. Go for the spectacle and the squab, not for a quiet dinner — and book, because the queue at dinner is real.

Top 10 must-try

Dianping 推荐菜, TOP 1–10 for the New World City branch — the ranking is by the app's like counts.

Source: Dianping 推荐菜 · TOP 1–10

  1. 招牌玻璃乳鸽
    Signature Glass-Skin Squab ¥129

    The top order: lacquered skin thin enough to see through, meat still pink at the bone. Eat it with your hands.

  2. 冲浪鲍鱼泡饭
    "Surfing" Abalone Rice ¥89

    Abalone and rice with hot broth poured at the table — the name is the pour. Let it sit a minute before eating.

  3. 实验中的水果茶
    "Experiment" Fruit Tea ¥49 Drink

    Served in lab glassware, sweet-sour and built for photographs. The most-ordered drink on the list.

  4. 茶香火焰肋排
    Tea-Smoked Flaming Ribs ¥89

    Lit at the table. The ribs are fried first so they stay crisp under the flame.

  5. 雪花牛肉
    Snowflake Beef ¥89

    Dry ice under the lid, marbled beef inside. The most filmed dish in the room.

  6. 原创红酒鹅肝烤鸭配蔬菜沙拉
    Red-Wine Foie Gras with Roast Duck and Salad ¥179

    The most expensive plate in the ten: roast duck, foie gras, red-wine reduction, a salad to cut it.

  7. 鹅肝面包塔
    Foie Gras Bread Tower ¥79 Starter

    Foie gras on toast with a little mustard — the starter to share. Rich enough that two bites each is enough.

  8. 黑松露拇指小煎包
    Black Truffle Thumb Buns ¥48 Dim sum

    Small enough to eat in one bite: crisp base, soft top, truffle on the finish.

  9. 捞汁小海鲜
    Seafood in Chilled Dressing ¥79 Cold

    Head-on prawns in a sour-savoury dressing. Order it cold, between the fried courses.

  10. 斑斓芒果布丁
    Pandan Mango Pudding ¥22 Dessert

    The dessert: strong mango, bright green, made for the last photo of the night.

Prices from the Dianping listing. Two set menus do most of the work here: the ¥290 two-person “creative fireworks” (built around the snowflake beef) and the ¥420 three-to-four-person roast duck set.
