Fu 1088 (福1088)

镇宁路

Changning · Shanghai

CuisineShanghainese (benbang) · 本幫菜
Price¥580–800 per person
★MICHELIN One StarCurrent Shanghai guide
珠Black Pearl — One Diamond2026 黑珍珠一钻
1No. 1 Shanghai Smoked FishDianping 菜品榜 · 王牌菜

Benbang cooking served in a 1920s villa, one private room at a time.

The house is one of three 1920s villas on Zhenning Road, collectively known as 福园. Original timber staircases, old window frames and patterned floor tiles were kept; the rooms were converted into private dining suites rather than knocked through into a single restaurant floor.

In 2005, 方元 joined the family business and rebuilt the property as 福1088, pairing a design background with a benbang kitchen. Two siblings followed — 福1039 and 福1015 — each in its own villa, each with a slightly different register.

The cooking is old Shanghai, cooked properly: braised pork belly that has been given time, river fish handled with restraint, and a kitchen willing to serve 青鱼秃肺 — the fat-rich swim bladder of black carp — which almost nobody outside the city orders.

Why it became famous

What the city, the guides and the queues actually agree on.

  • ◆A MICHELIN star and a Black Pearl diamond in the same house.
  • ◆Private-room-only service in a genuine 1920s villa — it feels like dinner in someone's home, because it was.
  • ◆One of the few restaurants still serving the older benbang repertoire, including 青鱼秃肺.
  • ◆Part of a three-villa family group (福1088 / 福1039 / 福1015) on the same street.

Top 10 must-try

The ten dishes that define the house — Chinese name first, English translation under it.

Source: Dianping 推荐菜 · only the top ten are listed

  1. 老上海熏鱼
    Old-Shanghai Smoked Fish ¥128 No. 1

    No. 1 on Dianping's Shanghai smoked-fish chart. Crisp outside, juicy inside, sweet-savoury rather than smoky.

  2. 清炒蟹粉配吐司
    Stir-Fried Crab Roe with Toast ¥398

    A full bowl of pure crab roe, baguette toasted and drizzled with aged vinegar. The most expensive plate on the list, and the one to order once.

  3. 香溢红烧肉
    Fragrant Red-Braised Pork Belly ¥228 Top 5

    No. 5 on the Shanghai red-braised-pork chart. The fat turns to jelly, the lean stays lean — order rice with it.

  4. 腰果苔条酥
    Cashew and Seaweed Pastry ¥38 Pastry

    Cashew with seaweed powder: a sweet-savoury pastry that should not work and does. The cheapest thing in the ten.

  5. 糖藕
    Sweet Lotus Root ¥58 Cold

    Crisp outside, soft within, honeyed through. Order it early — it is also the dessert.

  6. 黄鱼煨面
    Yellow Croaker Noodles ¥38 Noodles

    The broth is cooked down from fish bones for hours; the noodles are almost secondary to it.

  7. 沪式油爆虾
    Shanghai Flash-Fried River Shrimp ¥228

    Shells glossy and crisp enough to eat whole. The sugar in the glaze should stop just short of burnt.

  8. 鸡头米小豌豆
    Gorgon Nut and Baby Peas ¥88 Vegetable

    Sweet peas, springy gorgon nut — the palate cleanser between the heavy dishes.

  9. 响油鳝糊
    Sizzling Shredded Eel ¥188

    Hot oil poured at the table; white pepper is the finishing note. Stir before it cools.

  10. 芥末芝麻虾
    Mustard and Sesame Prawns ¥188 Cold

    Prawns plump, mustard restrained, sesame on the finish. Served cold.
