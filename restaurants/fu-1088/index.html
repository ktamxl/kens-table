Fu 1088 (福1088)

镇宁路

Changning · Shanghai

CuisineShanghainese (benbang) · 本幫菜
Price¥600–900 per person
★MICHELIN One StarCurrent Shanghai guide
珠Black Pearl — One Diamond黑珍珠一钻

Benbang cooking served in a 1920s villa, one private room at a time.

The house is one of three 1920s villas on Zhenning Road, collectively known as 福园. Original timber staircases, old window frames and patterned floor tiles were kept; the rooms were converted into private dining suites rather than knocked through into a single restaurant floor.

In 2005, 方元 joined the family business and rebuilt the property as 福1088, pairing a design background with a benbang kitchen. Two siblings followed — 福1039 and 福1015 — each in its own villa, each with a slightly different register.

The cooking is old Shanghai, cooked properly: braised pork belly that has been given time, river fish handled with restraint, and a kitchen willing to serve 青鱼秃肺 — the fat-rich swim bladder of black carp — which almost nobody outside the city orders.

Old Shanghai, served one room at a time.The house's own description of the format

Why it became famous

What the city, the guides and the queues actually agree on.

  • ◆A MICHELIN star and a Black Pearl diamond in the same house.
  • ◆Private-room-only service in a genuine 1920s villa — it feels like dinner in someone's home, because it was.
  • ◆One of the few restaurants still serving the older benbang repertoire, including 青鱼秃肺.
  • ◆Part of a three-villa family group (福1088 / 福1039 / 福1015) on the same street.

Signatures worth ordering

A short list of dishes the house is known for. The full top ten will follow Dianping's 推荐菜 ranking for this branch.

Why not ten yet: this post is on the list but has not been written up. Rather than invent a ranking, the page shows the dishes that are consistently cited — the top ten arrives, in order, from Dianping's 推荐菜 list when the feature is written.
  1. 青鱼秃肺
    Black Carp Swim Bladder Known signature

    The dish that proves the kitchen's bona fides. Rich, gelatinous, faintly sweet.

  2. 红烧肉
    Red-Braised Pork Belly Known signature

    Slow-braised, lacquered, served in a clay pot. The benchmark order.

  3. 油爆虾
    Flash-Fried River Shrimp Known signature

    Shells crisp enough to eat, sauce reduced to a glaze.

  4. 蟹粉豆腐
    Crab Roe with Tofu Known signature

    Seasonal; ask whether the crab is from Taihu before ordering.
