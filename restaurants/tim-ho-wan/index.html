Ken's Table/ All restaurants/ Hong Kong

Tim Ho Wan (添好運)

深水埗

Sham Shui Po · Hong Kong

CuisineDim sum · 點心
PriceHK$60–110 per person
★MICHELIN One StarFirst awarded 2010, the year after opening

The dim sum shop that took a MICHELIN star and kept the prices.

添好運 opened in 2009 on Fuk Wing Street, a short walk from Sham Shui Po MTR, with a chef — 麥桂培 — who had spent years making dim sum at Lung King Heen. The idea was a small shop doing a short menu properly, at prices that did not require a hotel.

A year later the MICHELIN Guide gave it a star. At the time it was described as the cheapest starred restaurant in the world, and the queue outside became part of the neighbourhood. The guide has moved on since, and the brand has expanded across Asia, but this shop is still the original: a few tables, a laminated order sheet, and a kitchen visible through the pass.

The 酥皮焗叉燒包 is the dish that made the reputation — a char siu bun with a sugar-crust top borrowed from Western baking, which nobody was doing in dim sum at the time. Order it first, because it is the one item that sells out.

A star, a queue, and a laminated menu.The Sham Shui Po original

Why it became famous

What the city, the guides and the queues actually agree on.

  • ◆MICHELIN one star in 2010 — at the time, the cheapest starred restaurant in the world.
  • ◆酥皮焗叉燒包 — the baked char siu bun with a crisp sugar crust.
  • ◆A short menu: roughly twenty items, done consistently, rather than a trolley of fifty.
  • ◆The original Sham Shui Po shop, still the one worth queueing for.

Top 10 must-try

The house's 'four kings' — baked char siu bun, liver rice roll, turnip cake, sponge cake — plus the dim sum regulars.

Source: OpenRice 必食 + house menu

  1. 酥皮焗叉燒包
    Baked Char Siu Bun with Crisp Crust

    The signature. Order two portions — one is never enough and they sell out.

  2. 黃沙豬潤腸
    Rice Roll with Pork Liver

    Silky roll, liver cooked just short of set. The best thing after the bun.

  3. 香煎蘿蔔糕
    Pan-Fried Turnip Cake

    Crisp outside, loose and peppery inside. Ask for it extra pan-fried if you like the edges.

  4. 香滑馬拉糕
    Steamed Sponge Cake

    Three days of fermentation in a single slice. Light, brown-sugar sweet.

  5. 晶瑩鮮蝦餃
    Shrimp Dumpling

    Translucent wrapper, whole shrimp. The baseline test of the kitchen.

  6. 鮮蝦燒賣皇
    Shrimp Siu Mai

    Pork and shrimp, no filler, a whole shrimp on top.

  7. 洛神花脆蝦紅米腸粉
    Hibiscus Crispy Shrimp Rice Roll

    The newer signature: red rice wrapper, crisp middle, shrimp.

  8. 鮮蝦腐皮卷
    Shrimp Bean-Curd Roll

    Fried to order; the wrapper shatters. Do not let it sit.

  9. 陳皮牛肉球
    Beef Ball with Dried Tangerine Peel

    Springy, faintly citrus. A test of the steaming rather than the frying.

  10. 豉汁蒸鳳爪
    Chicken Feet in Black-Bean Sauce

    Sticky, savoury, the thing to order while waiting for the baked items.
