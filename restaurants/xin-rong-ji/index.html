Xin Rong Ji (新荣记)

南京西路店

Nanjing West Road · Shanghai

CuisineTaizhou seafood (Zhejiang) · 苏浙菜・台州菜
Price¥680–1,200 per person
1No. 1 Su-Zhe Restaurant in ShanghaiDianping 热门榜
4.8Dianping 4.8 / 514,583 reviews at the time of listing
★MICHELIN-starred groupThe Beijing flagship has held three stars

Taizhou seafood at the top of the market — the pigeon is ¥98, the croaker is ¥2,280.

新荣记 started in 1995 as a seafood 大排档 in 临海, Taizhou, run by 张勇 — a cook who calls himself a cook rather than a restaurateur. Thirty years later it is the reference point for Taizhou cooking in China: more than forty rooms in Beijing, Shanghai, Shenzhen, Hong Kong and Tokyo, and a group that has been in the MICHELIN guide since Shanghai's first edition in 2016.

The cooking is Zhejiang coastal, not Shanghainese: yellow croaker braised with rice cakes, sand anemone folded through sweet-potato noodles, hairtail fried hard. It is a cuisine built on fish, salt and stock rather than sugar and soy, which is why it tastes unlike the rest of this list.

This branch has been on Nanjing West Road for eleven years, on the second floor of Henderson 688. It is a business-dinner room — private tables, a phone booking, and a menu where the prices run from ¥18 for a bun to ¥2,280 for a croaker. Ask the price of the fish before you order it; that is normal here.

Top 10 must-try

Dianping 推荐菜, TOP 1–10 for the Nanjing West Road branch — ranked by the app's like counts.

Source: Dianping 推荐菜 · TOP 1–10

  1. 脆皮乳鸽
    Crisp-Skin Pigeon ¥98 No. 1

    The top order. Skin thin enough to crack, juice that runs the moment you bite. The best value on a very expensive menu.

  2. 沙蒜烧豆面
    Sea Anemone with Sweet-Potato Noodles ¥328 Taizhou

    The Taizhou dish that made the restaurant: sand anemone braised into glassy noodles. Savoury, slippery, unlike anything else in Shanghai.

  3. 黄金脆带鱼
    Golden Crisp Hairtail (large) ¥298 Fried

    Hairtail fried until the edges curl. Crisp outside, juicy inside — order the large portion for four.

  4. 鲳鱼烧年糕
    Pomfret with Rice Cakes ¥558 Fish

    White pomfret soft enough to fall apart at a touch, rice cakes chewy underneath. The sauce is the point.

  5. 蜜汁红薯
    Honey-Glazed Sweet Potato ¥38 Sweet

    A whole sweet potato, roasted until it leaks sugar, then glazed. The cheapest thing on the list and the one people remember.

  6. 黄鱼
    Braised Yellow Croaker ¥2,280

    The signature, priced by size — ask before you order it. Fish, rice cakes, and a sauce thick enough to coat a spoon.

  7. 龙虾汤泡饭
    Lobster Broth Rice ¥198 Rice

    Rice in a reduced lobster broth. The dish to order when you want the kitchen's stock work without the fish price.

  8. 黑松露鹅肝包
    Black Truffle Foie Gras Bun ¥18 Dim sum

    A small baked bun that shatters, filled with truffle and foie gras. Order two each.

  9. 溏心富贵虾
    Mantis Shrimp with Runny Roe ¥1,280 Showpiece

    The showpiece: mantis shrimp with a molten roe centre, plated whole. Order it for the table, not for one.

  10. 桂花鸡头米
    Osmanthus and Gorgon Nut ¥68 Dessert

    Soft gorgon nut with lily bulb and longan, sweetened lightly. The dessert to finish a long dinner.

Prices from the Dianping listing. The croaker and the mantis shrimp are market-priced by size — confirm before ordering. Two people can eat well on the pigeon, the noodles and the sweet potato for under ¥500.
