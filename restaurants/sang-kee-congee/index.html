Sang Kee Congee Shop (生記粥品專家)

上環

Sheung Wan · Hong Kong

CuisineCongee · 粥品
PriceHK$50–90 per person
必MICHELIN Bib GourmandA forty-year-old Sheung Wan institution
Show this to the waiter畀侍應睇
魚腩粥，加豬紅，唔該。 魚腩粥，走豬紅，唔該。
Fish-belly congee — with pork blood, or without.

魚腩 is the belly cut, the fattest part of the fish. 豬紅 is pork blood curd; 走 means leave it out. Order either line as it is written.

Forty years of fish congee on Burd Street.

生記粥品專家 has been on Burd Street for over forty years, in a part of Sheung Wan that still looks like a working neighbourhood rather than a gallery district. The congee base is cooked down with pork bones, dried scallop and lean pork until the rice breaks, then the raw ingredients are added to order — the 生滾 method, where the heat of the congee does the cooking.

The specialty is fish: 魚腩粥, the belly cut, sliced and laid into the bowl before the congee goes over it. It arrives with a dish of ginger, spring onion and soy on the side, which you add yourself. The other house order is 鮮豬潤粥 — pork liver, barely cooked, still pink at the centre.

It holds a Bib Gourmand and has done for years. The room is bright, the tables are shared, and the pace is fast enough that nobody minds if you are still eating when the next party sits down.

The congee cooks the fish. That is the whole technique.How 生滾 works

Why it became famous

What the city, the guides and the queues actually agree on.

  • ◆魚腩粥 — fish-belly congee, the cut that makes the bowl.
  • ◆生滾 technique: raw fish and liver cooked by the heat of the congee, not in the pot.
  • ◆鯪魚球 — hand-made dace fish balls, and 香煎魚餅 on the side.
  • ◆A forty-year-old shop with a Bib Gourmand and neighbourhood prices.

Top 10 must-try

The shop's 生滾 congee list — fish first, liver second, everything cooked by the heat of the bowl.

Source: OpenRice 必食 + house menu

  1. 魚腩粥
    Fish-Belly Congee

    The order. Add the ginger and soy at the table, not before.

  2. 鮮豬潤粥
    Fresh Pork Liver Congee

    Liver added raw and poached by the congee; eat it before it overcooks.

  3. 鯪魚球粥
    Dace Fish Ball Congee

    Springy, faintly dried-tangerine. The non-fish-cut alternative.

  4. 魚片粥
    Fish Slice Congee

    Thin slices, no bones, the gentlest version of the fish congee.

  5. 牛肉粥
    Beef Congee

    Minced beef pressed into the bowl and cooked by the congee.

  6. 皮蛋瘦肉粥
    Century Egg and Pork Congee

    The safe order at a lesser shop; here it is the control.

  7. 香煎魚餅
    Pan-Fried Fish Cake

    Cut into strips, good with the ginger-soy dish.

  8. 炸兩
    Rice Roll Wrapped Around Fried Dough

    Ordered on the side for texture. Eat immediately.

  9. 油條
    Fried Dough Stick

    For dipping into the congee, or into the soy.

  10. 豆漿
    Soy Milk

    Cold or warm, unsweetened by default.
