Jintang Chunxiang (锦堂春想·广府粤菜)

深圳湾首店

Shenzhen Bay, Nanshan · Shenzhen

CuisineCantonese · 粤菜
Price¥290–500 per person

Cantonese luxury opposite Shenzhen Bay MixC — glass-skinned pigeon, and mantis shrimp at ¥796.

锦堂春想 sits on the fourth floor of the Dacheng Fund headquarters on Haide 3rd Road, directly opposite Shenzhen Bay MixC. It is three years old, has private rooms and a phone booking line, and prices itself for the business district around it: ¥290 a head before anyone orders the mantis shrimp.

The cooking is Cantonese with a luxury register — whole mantis shrimp with roe cooked to a molten centre, Sri Lankan crab, ten-year tangerine peel double-boiled with duck. The pigeon is the exception to the pricing: ¥69, glass-skinned, and ranked No. 6 in Shenzhen.

There are two mantis shrimp on the list, at ¥796 and ¥398. The difference is the size and the amount of roe; if you have not eaten it before, start with the Thai one and decide whether the upgrade is worth it.

One practical note: several dishes are priced by weight, so the bill can move. Ask before ordering the crab.

Top 10 must-try

Dianping 推荐菜, TOP 1–10 for the Shenzhen Bay flagship — ranked by the app's like counts, with the dish-chart positions where they exist.

Source: Dianping 推荐菜 · TOP 1–10

  1. 琥珀溏心富贵虾
    Amber Mantis Shrimp with Runny Roe ¥796 No. 1

    The top order and the most expensive thing here: roe cooked to a molten centre, meat sweet and springy. Order it for the table.

  2. 招牌玻璃脆皮乳鸽
    Signature Glass-Skin Pigeon ¥69 No. 6

    No. 6 on Shenzhen's pigeon chart. Skin thin as cellophane, meat still juicy — the best value on a luxury menu.

  3. 泰国有膏富贵虾
    Thai Mantis Shrimp with Roe ¥398 Seafood

    The half-price version of the same idea: roe softer, meat a little sweeter. Start here if the ¥796 is too much.

  4. 10年陈皮炖水鸭汤
    Ten-Year Tangerine Peel Duck Soup Soup

    Double-boiled with aged peel — warm, faintly bitter, sweet at the finish. The soup to order before anything fried.

  5. 斯里兰卡大青蟹
    Sri Lankan Mud Crab Seafood

    Cooked either simply or 避风塘 style with garlic and chilli. Ask which is better that day.

  6. 小青龙贵妃鲜虾泡饭
    Baby Lobster and Prawn Rice in Broth ¥143 Rice

    Rice in a shellfish broth with lobster and prawn. The cheapest way to taste the seafood kitchen.

  7. 明炉广式烧鹅
    Cantonese Roast Goose ¥129 Roast

    Black-bristle goose, roasted in the open oven. Skin crisp, fat rendered — order a quarter.

  8. 经典蚝豉焖猪手
    Braised Pork Knuckle with Dried Oyster Braised

    Collagen-rich, savoury with dried oyster. A Cantonese banquet dish, priced for sharing.

  9. 怀旧五柳炸蛋
    Old-School Five-Willow Fried Egg Nostalgia

    Whites fried crisp, yolk still runny, sweet-sour sauce underneath. A nostalgic dish done properly.

  10. 黄贝
    Steamed Yellow Clams with Vermicelli ¥43 Shellfish

    Clams steamed with glass noodles and garlic. The cheap order that makes the table feel complete.

Prices from the Dianping listing. Items without a price are market-priced by weight — confirm at the table. The pigeon and the baby-lobster rice are the two orders that keep the bill sane.
