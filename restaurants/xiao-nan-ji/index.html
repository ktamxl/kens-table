Xiao Nan Ji (小煵记·深圳本地菜)

南新店

Nanshan centre · Shenzhen

CuisineShenzhen local Cantonese · 粤菜・深圳本地菜
Price¥120–180 per person
必Dianping Must-Eat List 20262026 上榜餐厅
4.4Dianping 4.4 / 512,799 reviews at the time of listing

Seventeen years of Shenzhen local cooking, open until midnight, on the 2026 Must-Eat List.

小煵记 has been cooking what it calls 深圳本地菜 for seventeen years — Shenzhen local food, which in practice means old Cantonese with a bit of Hakka and Chaozhou in it, cooked in a big, loud room that stays open until midnight.

The menu is built for sharing and for beer: soy-sauce prawns, salt-and-pepper nine-bar fish, black-bean chicken in a claypot, salt-baked baby crab. Nothing is plated for a photograph; everything is plated for a table of six.

It made the 2026 Must-Eat List, and the volume tells its own story — 12,799 reviews at 4.4, with the iced lemon tea outselling every dish (9,000 in six months). That is what a neighbourhood institution looks like: not a destination, a habit.

One practical thing: the room is busy from 6pm and stays busy. Go late rather than early.

Top 10 must-try

Dianping 推荐菜, TOP 1–10 for the Nanxin Road branch — ranked by the app's like counts.

Source: Dianping 推荐菜 · TOP 1–10

  1. 豉油皇大虾
    Prawns in Master Soy Sauce ¥94 No. 1

    The top order — 135 likes and 7,000 sold in six months. Shell-on, wok-heavy, sweet at the edges; eat them with your hands.

  2. 椒盐九肚鱼
    Salt-and-Pepper Bombay Duck ¥39 No. 2

    Nine-bar fish fried in a salt-and-pepper crust, soft enough to eat without noticing the bones. 5,000 sold in six months.

  3. 豆豉鸡
    Black-Bean Chicken ¥88 No. 3

    Claypot chicken with fermented black beans — skin caramelised, meat still juicy. The most Cantonese thing on the menu.

  4. 冻柠茶
    Iced Lemon Tea ¥18 Drink

    9,000 sold in six months, more than any dish. Strong tea, lemon, crushed ice — the reason people come at midnight.

  5. 炒圣子皇
    Wok-Fried Razor Clams ¥98 Wok

    Fat clams, no grit, ginger and scallion. A test of the kitchen's seafood handling.

  6. 盐焗奄仔蟹
    Salt-Baked Baby Mud Crab ¥138 Crab

    Young crab baked in coarse salt; the roe turns to liquid butter. Order it in season.

  7. 咕噜肉
    Sweet-and-Sour Pork ¥68 Classic

    Crisp outside, sweet-sour balanced rather than cloying. The dish that tells you the wok is hot.

  8. 椒丝腐乳通菜
    Water Spinach with Chilli and Fermented Bean Curd Vegetable

    Morning glory fried with fermented bean curd — funky, salty, the correct vegetable for this table.

  9. 芥兰炒双宝
    Gai Lan with Two Delicacies ¥68 Vegetable

    Chinese broccoli with two kinds of offal, wok-fried until the stems squeak.

  10. 南记煎鸭
    Nan Ji Pan-Fried Duck ¥188 Legacy

    The house legacy dish: duck braised then pan-fried, dry outside rather than fatty. Order it for the table.

Prices from the Dianping listing; the water spinach is market-priced. The set menus are the value: ¥190 for the two-person 本地炒菜 set and ¥390 for four. The 50-yuan voucher sells for ¥40.5.
