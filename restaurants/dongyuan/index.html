Dongyuan (东园·广州味·点心与烧鹅)

天娱广场店

Shipai / Longkou, Tianhe · Guangzhou

CuisineGuangzhou Cantonese · 广州菜
Price¥90–130 per person
1No. 1 Guangzhou Restaurant in TianheDianping 回头客榜 — repeat customers
4.8Dianping 4.8 / 510,303 reviews at the time of listing

Tile-kiln roast goose with a rule: not sold more than 45 minutes out of the oven.

东园 is on the eleventh floor of Tianyu Plaza's west tower, forty metres from Gangding station — an unlikely address for a roast goose specialist until you get out of the lift and the whole floor smells of the kiln.

The banner makes two claims: 瓦窑烧鹅, tile-kiln roast goose, and 出炉超45分不卖 — nothing sold more than 45 minutes after it leaves the oven. That second rule is worth more than a star: it is why the skin still crackles when the plate reaches you.

It has been open three years and is No. 1 in Tianhe for repeat customers, with 4.8 across 10,303 reviews at ¥90 a head. Morning tea runs from 08:30, dinner from 17:00; the goose is the constant.

The rest of the menu is Guangzhou home cooking with a few playful notes — guava and green plum on the prawns, mint and strawberry on the char siu, a koi-shaped jelly in the mango sago. The kitchen is having fun; the classics are still done straight.

Top 10 must-try

Dianping 推荐菜, TOP 1–10 for the Tianyu Plaza branch — ranked by the app's like counts.

Source: Dianping 推荐菜 · TOP 1–10

  1. 东园果木烧鹅
    Dongyuan Fruitwood Roast Goose ¥88 No. 1

    4,540 likes — the most-liked dish on any page in this list. Fruitwood-roasted, skin crackling, meat juicy; the plum sauce cuts the fat.

  2. 大师傅生炒宁夏菜心
    Wok-Fried Ningxia Choy Sum ¥48 No. 2

    Stir-fried rather than blanched, so the stems stay crisp and the leaves char slightly. The best vegetable order in the room.

  3. 缤纷四季咕噜肉
    Four-Season Sweet-and-Sour Pork ¥59 No. 3

    Crisp pork with seasonal fruit — pineapple, strawberry, whatever is in. Sharper and less sugary than the classic.

  4. 红芭乐青梅焗罗氏虾
    Baked Prawns with Guava and Green Plum ¥98 No. 4

    Prawns baked under a fruit-sour glaze. The oddest dish on the list and one of the best — order it if you like sharp flavours.

  5. 脆椒一口牛
    Crisp Chilli Beef Cubes ¥98 No. 5

    Beef cubes with dried chilli and asparagus, cooked fast so the inside stays soft. Eats like a steak, costs like a stir-fry.

  6. 奶酪烤贝贝瓜
    Baked Baby Pumpkin with Cheese ¥26 No. 6

    Sweet pumpkin under melted cheese. Sounds wrong, works — the cheapest thing on the list and the one children fight over.

  7. 锦鲤阿达子杨枝甘露
    Koi Mango Pomelo Sago ¥28 No. 7

    Mango pomelo sago with a koi-shaped jelly. The dessert the room photographs; the mango is genuinely good.

  8. 葱蒜油浸笋壳鱼
    Fish Poached in Scallion and Garlic Oil ¥179 No. 8

    Whole fish in hot scallion oil — crisp outside, soft inside, the oil carrying the aromatics. The most expensive savoury dish here.

  9. 薄荷黑金叉烧
    Mint and Black-Gold Char Siu ¥79 No. 9

    Char siu glazed dark and finished with mint and strawberry. A gimmick that earns its place — the mint cuts the glaze.

  10. 东园果木烧鹅拼玉米
    Roast Goose with Sweetcorn ¥106 Duplicate

    The goose again, plated with corn — the app lists it separately, but it is the same bird. Order the plain goose unless you want the corn.

Prices from the Dianping listing. The sets are the value: ¥172 for the two-to-three-person 东园豪华餐, ¥250 for the four-person autumn tasting menu, and ¥378 for the four-person dim sum set (valid at all branches).
