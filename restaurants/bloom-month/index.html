Bloom Month (花月冰室)

尖沙咀北京道

Tsim Sha Tsui · Hong Kong

CuisineHong Kong cha chaan teng · 冰室・茶餐廳
PriceHK$60–150 per person
1No. 1 Pork Chop Rice in Hong KongDianping 菜品榜, 2026
3No. 3 Toast in Hong KongDianping 菜品榜 — the 西多士

A Tsim Sha Tsui bing sutt where the rice plates outrank the toast.

花月冰室 opened on Peking Road in 2025, in the middle of the Tsim Sha Tsui hotel district — an odd place for a cheap lunch and a good one for a break from it. It is a 冰室 in form: tiled walls, booth seating, a menu that runs from breakfast to late.

What makes it worth a page is the range. The core is tea-shop food, but the kitchen also runs satay beef noodles, curry pork chop, bak kut teh and Hainanese chicken — the Southeast Asian side of the Hong Kong menu that most bing sutt have dropped.

The egg dishes are the strength: 滑蛋, scrambled until barely set and poured over rice, with beef or eel on top. That is where the top of the ranking sits, and it is what to order on a first visit.

Dianping users have ranked it unusually well in specific categories: No. 1 pork chop rice in Hong Kong, No. 3 toast, No. 5 milk tea, No. 9 bak kut teh. Four category leaders on one menu is rare for a shop this young.

Why it became famous

What the city, the guides and the queues actually agree on.

  • ◆No. 1 pork chop rice in Hong Kong on Dianping's dish ranking.
  • ◆Also top-five for milk tea and top-three for toast — unusual breadth for one shop.
  • ◆The 滑蛋 rice plates: beef and spring onion, or a whole eel fillet, over half-set egg.
  • ◆Southeast Asian dishes (satay noodles, curry, bak kut teh) that most bing sutt no longer bother with.

Top 10 must-try

Dianping 推荐菜, TOP 1–10 for the Peking Road shop — the ranking is by the app's like counts.

Source: Dianping 推荐菜 · TOP 1–10

  1. 蔥花牛肉滑蛋飯
    Scrambled Egg Rice with Beef and Spring Onion HK$63

    The top order by a distance — 969 likes. Egg barely set, beef seared hard outside and still soft in the middle.

  2. 鮮茄菠蘿焗豬扒飯
    Baked Pork Chop Rice with Fresh Tomato and Pineapple HK$72

    Ranked No. 1 for pork chop rice in Hong Kong on Dianping. Cheese pulls, tomato cooked right down, pork chop thick-cut.

  3. 凍奶茶
    Iced Milk Tea

    Ranked No. 5 for milk tea in Hong Kong. Cold, smooth, a strip of lemon peel in the glass.

  4. 西多士
    French Toast HK$38

    Ranked No. 3 for toast. Crisp outside, soft inside, a slab of butter melting into the middle.

  5. 鰻魚滑蛋飯
    Grilled Eel with Scrambled Egg Rice HK$98

    A whole unagi fillet laid over half-set egg. The most generous plate on the list.

  6. 咖喱豬扒
    Curry Pork Chop HK$72

    Thick pork, Hong Kong-style curry, no apology about the sauce. The classic tea-shop order.

  7. 招牌沙嗲牛肉即食麵
    Signature Satay Beef Instant Noodles HK$42

    Beef braised until it gives way, thick satay broth. The cheap order regulars default to.

  8. 醬爆爆魚雞煲
    Sizzling Chicken Pot with Abalone HK$148

    The most expensive thing in the ten. Chicken stays tender; the abalone is bigger than you expect.

  9. 咖喱飯
    Beef Rib Curry Rice HK$72

    Short rib, soft enough to cut with a spoon, over rice. Order it if you want the curry without the pork.

  10. 秘製肉骨茶
    Secret-Recipe Bak Kut Teh HK$82

    Ranked No. 9 for bak kut teh in Hong Kong. Herbal rather than peppery, and a very large bowl.

Prices from the Dianping listing, in HK$ (the RMB figure in the app is their conversion). Just outside the ten: 無骨海南雞 HK$78 and 瑤柱蛋白炒飯 HK$65.
