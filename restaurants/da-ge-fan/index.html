Da Ge Fan (大鸽饭)

福田 COCO Park 店

Shopping Park, Futian · Shenzhen

CuisineCantonese pigeon specialist · 粤菜・鸽
Price¥120–170 per person
1No. 1 Restaurant in the COCO Park Food ChartDianping 热门榜
4.8Dianping 4.8 / 513,303 reviews at the time of listing

Pigeon three ways, from a restaurant with its own pigeon farm.

大鸽饭 does one thing and does it in quantity: pigeon. The banner in the shop promises a 自有鸽场 — its own pigeon farm — with birds killed daily, which is why the skin is still tight and the meat has not started to loosen.

There are three classic preparations: 红烧 (braised and glazed), 盐焗 (baked in coarse salt) and 生炸 (deep-fried). Each comes in a 鸽皇 size at ¥59 and, for the salt-baked version, a smaller ¥23 bird. Order two preparations between three people and compare — that is the point of the place.

The rice is the other half of the name. 大鸽饭 at ¥19 is rice cooked with pigeon fat and a little meat; 黑松露大鸽饭 at ¥69 adds truffle and a claypot crust worth fighting over. The 鸽汤泡饭 at ¥89 is the gentler option — milky broth poured over rice.

It is a mall restaurant on the third floor of COCO Park, three years old, and currently No. 1 in the complex's food chart. Book, or go early — the queue starts before service.

Top 10 must-try

Dianping 推荐菜, TOP 1–10 for the Futian COCO Park branch — ranked by the app's like counts.

Source: Dianping 推荐菜 · TOP 1–10

  1. 爆汁红烧鸽皇
    Juicy Red-Braised Pigeon ¥59 No. 1

    The dish with 590 likes — by far the most-ordered thing here. Skin lacquered, juice running the moment you bite. Eat it with your hands.

  2. 黑松露大鸽饭
    Black Truffle Pigeon Rice ¥69 No. 2

    Claypot rice with pigeon and truffle, mixed at the table. The truffle is a gimmick that works; the crust at the bottom is the real prize.

  3. 传统盐焗鸽皇
    Traditional Salt-Baked Pigeon ¥59 No. 3

    Baked in coarse salt, skin crackling, meat falling off the bone. The purist's order of the three.

  4. 大鸽饭
    Pigeon Rice ¥19 No. 4

    The namesake, and the cheapest way in: rice cooked with pigeon fat and a little meat. Order it alongside a whole bird.

  5. 传统生炸红烧鸽
    Traditional Deep-Fried Red Pigeon ¥59 No. 5

    Fried rather than braised — drier skin, deeper colour, more crunch. The one to order if you like texture over juice.

  6. 红葱头焗排骨
    Baked Pork Ribs with Shallots ¥69 No. 6

    The non-pigeon dish everyone orders. Shallots cook down into the ribs until everything is sweet and soft.

  7. 鸽汤泡饭
    Pigeon Broth Rice ¥89 No. 7

    Milky pigeon broth poured over rice. Gentle, restorative, and the thing to order after two fried birds.

  8. 经典盐焗鸽
    Classic Salt-Baked Pigeon (smaller) ¥23 No. 8

    The smaller salt-baked bird. Order it if there are two of you and you want a third preparation without committing to a 鸽皇.

  9. 菜心炒鸽杂
    Choy Sum with Pigeon Offal ¥56 No. 9

    Liver, gizzard and heart wok-fried with greens. The dish that proves the farm is real — nothing is wasted.

  10. 和味爽鱼皮
    Chilled Fish Skin in Soy Dressing ¥36 No. 10

    Cold, crunchy fish skin with mustard and soy. The palate reset between the pigeons.

Prices from the Dianping listing. A whole pigeon feeds one; three people should order two birds, one rice and one vegetable. The 100-yuan voucher is usually available in the app.
