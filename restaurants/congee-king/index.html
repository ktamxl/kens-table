Congee King (靠得住)

灣仔

Wan Chai · Hong Kong

CuisineCongee and noodles · 粥麵
PriceHK$60–110 per person
必MICHELIN Bib GourmandFifteen consecutive years, 2011–2025

Congee built on fish stock, not plain water — fifteen years of Bib Gourmand.

靠得住 has been in Wan Chai long enough to be part of the furniture, and its name is a small joke: 靠得住 means "you can rely on it". The kitchen backs that up with a congee base made from fish stock rather than water — the reason a bowl here tastes of the sea rather than of rice alone.

The house bowl is 心肝寶貝粥, a name that puns on the ingredients: 心 and 肝 for the liver, 寶貝 for the scallop. Pork liver sliced thin, a scallop, rice cooked until it collapses. It arrives fast and costs what a bowl of congee should.

The rest of the menu is the Cantonese noodle-shop repertoire — wonton noodles with a clean shrimp broth, noodles without alkaline bite, a few small plates. It has held a Bib Gourmand for fifteen consecutive years, which for a shop of this size is close to unheard of.

靠得住 — "you can rely on it." The name is the promise.The house name, translated

Why it became famous

What the city, the guides and the queues actually agree on.

  • ◆Fish-stock congee base — richer and more savoury than the standard water-and-rice version.
  • ◆心肝寶貝粥: pork liver and scallop, the bowl the guide recommends.
  • ◆Wonton noodles with a properly clean broth and no alkaline aftertaste.
  • ◆Fifteen consecutive Bib Gourmand years (2011–2025).

Top 10 must-try

OpenRice 必食 for the Wan Chai shop, led by the fish-stock congee.

Source: OpenRice 必食 + house menu

  1. 心肝寶貝粥
    Pork Liver and Scallop Congee

    The signature. Liver sliced thin so it poaches in the residual heat; scallop for sweetness.

  2. 豬膶粥
    Pork Liver Congee

    Straight liver, no scallop. Order this if you want to taste the congee base on its own terms.

  3. 豬膶魚腩粥
    Pork Liver and Fish-Belly Congee

    The combination regulars order — liver and the fattest cut of the fish in one bowl.

  4. 魚腩粥
    Fish-Belly Congee

    The other house standard, built on the same fish-stock base.

  5. 招牌魚皮
    Signature Fish Skin

    Crisp fried skin, served separately so it stays crisp. Eat it before it sinks.

  6. 鮮蝦雲吞麵
    Fresh Shrimp Wonton Noodles

    Thin noodles, whole shrimp, broth that does not need chilli.

  7. 炒麵
    Fried Noodles

    The savoury side order. Ask for it crisp at the edges.

  8. 腸粉
    Rice Noodle Roll

    Plain or with shrimp; the test is the texture of the roll, not the filling.

  9. 滋味燻魚
    Smoked Fish

    Sweet, dark, served cold — a Shanghainese dish this Cantonese kitchen does well.

  10. 白粥
    Plain Congee

    The control. If the plain version is good, everything else follows.
