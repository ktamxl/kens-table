Xu Ji Seafood (徐记海鲜·始于1999)

科技园店

Science Park, Nanshan · Shenzhen

CuisineSeafood · 海鲜
Price¥220–350 per person
1No. 1 Seafood Restaurant for Environment, Science ParkDianping 环境榜
4.9Dianping 4.9 / 53,958 reviews at the time of listing

A seafood chain that started in 1999, doing Cantonese-style fish in a Science Park office block.

徐记海鲜 has been running since 1999 and is now a chain — the kind of seafood restaurant that builds its menu around a tank room rather than a fixed list. This branch occupies the first two floors of the Fu'an Technology Building in Science Park, and it is the top-ranked seafood room in the district for environment.

The cooking is Cantonese-leaning: whole fish steamed, clams wok-fried with ginger and scallion, crab over glass noodles. The prices are Hong Kong-style — you pay for the fish you point at — but the floor is set up for business lunches, with private rooms and free parking.

The order to know about is 船家海鱼焗饭 at ¥78: rice baked in a claypot with sea fish, the crust scraped up at the end. It is the cheapest way to eat well here, and it is the top-ranked dish on the app.

Two value notes: the ¥38 Zhangzidao scallops are palm-sized, and the ¥36 walnut bun is the dessert everyone photographs.

Top 10 must-try

Dianping 推荐菜, TOP 1–10 for the Science Park branch — ranked by the app's like counts.

Source: Dianping 推荐菜 · TOP 1–10

  1. 船家海鱼焗饭
    Boatman's Baked Rice with Sea Fish ¥78 No. 1

    The top order: rice baked in a claypot with fish and its juices. The crust at the bottom is the reason to order it.

  2. 清蒸老虎斑
    Steamed Tiger Grouper ¥168 Fish

    Whole fish, ginger and scallion, hot oil at the end. The test of a seafood kitchen, and the safest expensive order.

  3. 姜葱炒东海圣子
    Wok-Fried Razor Clams with Ginger and Scallion ¥98 Wok

    Big clams, fast wok, ginger and scallion rather than sauce. Eat them while they are still hot enough to sting.

  4. 蒜蓉粉丝蒸松叶蟹
    Steamed Spider Crab with Garlic and Vermicelli ¥368 Crab

    Crab over glass noodles that soak up everything. The best value among the big seafood plates.

  5. 徐记辣酒煮花螺
    Spicy Wine-Braised Sea Snails ¥168 Spicy

    Snails in a red, chilli-heavy broth with cooking wine. Order it with beer, not tea.

  6. 招牌核桃包
    Signature Walnut Bun ¥36 Dessert

    A steamed bun shaped like a walnut, filled with walnut and white chocolate. The dessert that arrives before you ask.

  7. 脆皮咸香鸡
    Crisp-Skin Salted Chicken ¥188 Roast

    Skin crackles when you bite it, meat stays juicy. Half a bird for three.

  8. 堂灼鱼汤鲜贝
    Scallops Poached in Fish Broth ¥58 Soup

    Hot fish broth poured over raw scallop at the table. The broth tastes strongly of crucian carp — in a good way.

  9. 拍蒜紫苏炒金牌龙虾
    Lobster with Garlic and Perilla ¥338 Crab

    Lobster wok-fried with smashed garlic and perilla. The perilla is what makes it different from every other lobster on the coast.

  10. 蒜茸粉丝蒸獐子岛元贝
    Steamed Zhangzidao Scallops with Garlic and Vermicelli ¥38 Shellfish

    Palm-sized scallops under golden garlic and glass noodles. At ¥38, the bargain of the menu.

Prices from the Dianping listing. Live seafood is priced by weight — confirm before it goes in the tank bag. The ¥360 set for two (海鱼焗饭鱼鲜双人餐) is the value order.
