Yuejinghui Seafood Restaurant (悦景汇海鲜酒家·粤菜·早茶)

深圳首店

Science Park, Nanshan · Shenzhen

CuisineCantonese seafood and morning tea · 粤菜・早茶
Price¥240–350 per person
4.7Dianping 4.7 / 53,343 reviews at the time of listing

Morning tea and banquet seafood on the second floor of a Nanshan office tower.

悦景汇 occupies the second and third floors of the Caixun Technology Building in Nanshan, ninety metres from Yuehaimen station. It is a proper Cantonese 酒家: white cloths, private rooms, ten-person tables, and a morning-tea service that runs from 09:30 to 15:00 before the seafood dinner shift begins.

The menu is a banquet menu, which means it is wider than it is deep. Cantonese classics — 红烧乳鸽, 秘制叉烧, 虾饺 — sit next to a Chaoshan clam dish, a Hunan stir-fry, and a Beijing-style fruitwood duck carved at the table. That range is normal for a room built to host family lunches and company dinners.

The dim sum is the reason to come early: the shrimp dumpling is ¥29 and properly made, and the crisp-topped char siu bun is worth the mess. In the evening the duck and the herbal chicken take over.

One quirk: the Dianping list includes the fruitwood duck twice, as 烤鸭 and 片皮鸭. It is one order — carved at the table — so do not order both.

Why it became famous

What the city, the guides and the queues actually agree on.

  • ◆Morning tea from 09:30 to 15:00, in a district where that is rare.
  • ◆4.7 across 3,343 reviews — a banquet room that still satisfies à la carte diners.
  • ◆Fruitwood duck carved at the table, and a ¥29 shrimp dumpling that punches above it.
  • ◆A genuinely wide menu: Cantonese, Chaoshan, Hunan and Beijing in one kitchen.

Top 10 must-try

Dianping 推荐菜, TOP 1–10 for the Shenzhen first store — ranked by the app's like counts.

Source: Dianping 推荐菜 · TOP 1–10

  1. 红烧乳鸽
    Red-Braised Pigeon ¥68 No. 1

    The top order: braised then glazed, skin sticky, meat still pink. The best value on a banquet menu.

  2. 悦景汇虾饺皇
    Yuejinghui Shrimp Dumpling ¥29 No. 2

    Wrapper translucent enough to see the shrimp through it. Three to a basket — order two baskets.

  3. 秘制叉烧
    Secret-Recipe Char Siu ¥128 No. 3

    Thick-cut, charred at the edges, honeyed but not sweet. The dish to judge the barbecue section by.

  4. 金不换炒薄壳
    Wok-Fried Clams with Thai Basil ¥68 No. 4

    A Chaoshan dish — tiny clams, Thai basil, a lot of wok heat. Order it with beer.

  5. 农家小炒肉
    Farmhouse Stir-Fried Pork ¥68 No. 5

    Hunan-style, chilli-heavy, thin pork with green peppers. The one non-Cantonese dish everyone orders.

  6. 酥皮叉烧包
    Crisp-Topped Char Siu Bun ¥84 No. 6

    A thousand-layer crust over char siu — the pastry shatters when you pick it up. Share, or wear it.

  7. 果木烤鸭
    Fruitwood Roast Duck ¥238 No. 7

    Carved at the table, skin first. The room's showpiece and the reason to book a big table.

  8. 药膳水晶鸡
    Herbal Crystal Chicken ¥256 No. 8

    Poached chicken in a herb broth, served cold. Skin springy, meat faintly medicinal — in the right way.

  9. 西柚汁焗生蚝
    Baked Oyster with Grapefruit Juice ¥69 No. 9

    Oyster baked under a citrus crust. The acidity cuts the cheese and the oyster's richness.

  10. 果木烤片皮鸭
    Fruitwood Sliced Duck (same duck, second listing) ¥238 Duplicate

    The app lists the duck twice, once as 烤鸭 and once as 片皮鸭. It is one order, carved at the table — do not order both.

Prices from the Dianping listing. The set menus are the value: ¥180 for the two-to-three-person Chaozhou set, ¥480 for the four-person dim sum set, and ¥3,680 for the ten-person banquet. The 100-yuan voucher sells for ¥84.
