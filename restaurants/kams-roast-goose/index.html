Kam's Roast Goose (甘牌燒鵝)

灣仔

Wan Chai · Hong Kong

CuisineCantonese roast meats · 粵式燒味
PriceHK$150–300 per person
★MICHELIN One StarAwarded 2015, held every year since

Third-generation Yung Kee family, a Michelin star in under a year.

In 1942, 甘穗煇 opened 鏞記酒家 in Central. Over the next seventy years it became the restaurant the world used as shorthand for Cantonese roast goose — and, in the 2010s, the centre of a very public family dispute over its ownership.

In 2014, the third generation left and opened their own shop in Wan Chai: 甘牌燒鵝, run by 甘崇轅 (Hardy Kam). It was a small room on Hennessy Road with a glass window of hanging birds, a queue system borrowed from a bakery, and the family's marinade recipe.

Four months after opening, the MICHELIN Guide gave it a star. It has held that star every year since — one of the longest unbroken runs for a Hong Kong roast-meat shop, and an unusually fast ascent for a restaurant with no dining-room ambitions at all.

Grandfather's marinade, grandson's queue.The shorthand every Hong Kong food writer uses

Why it became famous

What the city, the guides and the queues actually agree on.

  • ◆Direct family line to Yung Kee (est. 1942): the same marinade logic, a much smaller room.
  • ◆MICHELIN star within four months of opening, retained for a decade.
  • ◆Goose is roasted in tight batches all afternoon, so the skin never has time to soften.
  • ◆Cash-and-queue operation: no reservations, no ceremony, very fast turnover.

Top 10 must-try

OpenRice 必食 for the Wan Chai shop — roast meats, cut to order.

Source: OpenRice 必食 + house menu

  1. 甘氏燒鵝
    Kam's Roast Goose

    A quarter or half. Ask for a little sauce on the side rather than poured over the skin.

  2. 燒鵝脾
    Roast Goose Leg

    The premium cut — darker, fattier, best eaten with your hands.

  3. 燒乳豬
    Roast Suckling Pig

    Sold by portion. The crackling is the reason to add it to a goose order.

  4. 滷水鵝肝
    Braised Goose Liver

    Silky, tea-dark, served cool. The best value on the menu.

  5. 滷水鵝掌翼
    Braised Goose Webs and Wings

    The plate to order while you wait.

  6. 蜜汁叉燒
    Honey Char Siu

    Ask for 半肥瘦 — half fat, half lean — or you will get the dry end.

  7. 油雞
    Soy-Sauce Poached Chicken

    Cold, glossy, ginger-scallion on the side.

  8. 脆皮燒肉
    Crispy Roast Pork

    Ordered as an add-on to the goose, not instead of it.

  9. 鵝油撈麵
    Noodles Tossed in Goose Fat

    The carbohydrate answer to a plate of roast meat.

  10. 老火例湯
    Slow-Boiled Soup of the Day

    Usually pork bone and winter melon. Included with most rice plates.
