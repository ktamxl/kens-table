Lei Garden (利苑酒家)

宜安广场店

Jianshe 6th Road, Yuexiu · Guangzhou

CuisineCantonese · 广州菜・粤菜
Price¥230–350 per person
珠Black Pearl — One Diamond2026 黑珍珠一钻餐厅
3No. 3 Pigeon in GuangzhouDianping 王牌菜 · 菜品榜
4.8Dianping 4.8 / 513,035 reviews at the time of listing

Twenty-two years on Jianshe 6th Road, one Black Pearl diamond, and a pigeon ranked third in the city.

利苑 is one of the names that defined modern Cantonese dining: founded in Hong Kong in 1973, it built its reputation on dim sum made to a standard rather than to a price, and on a kitchen that treats roast meats as a discipline. The Guangzhou rooms followed, and this one on Jianshe 6th Road has been listed for twenty-two years.

It holds a Black Pearl diamond as of 2026, and 4.8 across 13,035 reviews — numbers that are hard to sustain in a city that opens new Cantonese rooms every month. The dining room is a proper 酒家: white cloths, private rooms, ten-person tables, free parking.

The menu is the Cantonese canon done properly: 贵妃海鲜泡饭, roast pigeon ranked third in Guangzhou, three-layer pork with glass-crisp skin, old-fire soup, and a 佛跳墙 at ¥468 for the table. The pastry section is unusually strong for a savoury restaurant — the cheese-and-blueberry napoleon has 322 saves.

One quirk: the app lists the three-layer pork twice, as 冰皮 and 冰烧. It is the same cut — order one.

Why it became famous

What the city, the guides and the queues actually agree on.

  • ◆Black Pearl one diamond (2026) and twenty-two years at the same address.
  • ◆Roast pigeon ranked No. 3 in Guangzhou.
  • ◆Three-layer pork with skin that crackles — the benchmark for Cantonese roast pork.
  • ◆A pastry section strong enough to carry dessert: the napoleon and the turnip puff.

Top 10 must-try

Dianping 推荐菜, TOP 1–10 for the Yi'an Plaza branch — ranked by the app's like counts, with dish-chart positions where they exist.

Source: Dianping 推荐菜 · TOP 1–10

  1. 贵妃海鲜泡饭
    Seafood Rice in Golden Broth ¥160 No. 1

    The top order: rice in a golden shellfish broth, prawn and crab sweetness doing the work. Ask for the crisp rice on the side.

  2. 芝士蓝莓拿破仑
    Cheese and Blueberry Napoleon ¥38 No. 2

    The pastry everyone photographs — shatters without collapsing, cream cheese and blueberry inside. Order one to share.

  3. 乳鸽
    Roast Pigeon ¥110 No. 3

    No. 3 on Guangzhou's pigeon chart. Skin like glass, meat still juicy — the reason to book a table rather than order takeaway.

  4. 冰皮三层肉
    Ice-Skin Three-Layer Pork ¥130 No. 4

    Crisp skin, three distinct layers of fat and lean. The Cantonese roast pork benchmark, and the dish the kitchen is judged by.

  5. 杨枝甘露配沙琪玛
    Mango Pomelo Sago with Sachima ¥52 No. 5

    The dessert course: cold mango, pomelo, sago, with a piece of sachima for crunch. Order it before you are full.

  6. 冰烧三层肉
    Ice-Roasted Three-Layer Pork ¥130 Duplicate

    The same cut, listed a second time under its other name. One order is enough — do not let the app talk you into two.

  7. 银萝千层酥
    Turnip Puff Pastry ¥35 No. 7

    A thousand-layer pastry around turnip. Savoury, flaky, and the cheapest thing on the list.

  8. 老火靓汤
    Slow-Boiled Soup of the Day ¥42 No. 8

    The soup that tells you the kitchen is Cantonese: four hours, no shortcuts, no stock powder. Ask what it is that day.

  9. 烤乳鸭
    Roast Baby Duck ¥120 No. 9

    Smaller and richer than the pigeon — crisp skin, a little more fat. Order it instead of the pigeon if you want more meat.

  10. 佛跳墙
    Buddha Jumps Over the Wall ¥468 No. 10

    The banquet dish: abalone, fish maw, sea cucumber and more, in a thick brown broth. Order it for the table, not for one.

Prices from the Dianping listing. The Black Pearl tasting menus are the value if you want the full range: ¥398 and ¥698 per person. The two three-layer pork entries are one dish — order once.
