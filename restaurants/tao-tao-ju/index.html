Tao Tao Ju (陶陶居酒家)

第十甫路总店

Shangxiajiu, Liwan · Guangzhou

CuisineCantonese teahouse, dim sum · 粤式茶点
Price¥102 per person
必Dianping Must-Eat List 20262026 必吃榜 · 连续 8 年上榜
3No. 3 Prawn Dumpling in GuangzhouDianping 王牌菜 · 广州虾饺榜
米Michelin Guangzhou Recommendation连续 5 年 · 广州美食地标

Guangzhou's oldest teahouse, still hand-making dim sum at 146 years old — and 45,000 people have reviewed it.

陶陶居 opened in 1880 on 第十甫路 in Xiguan, and it is the oldest teahouse still trading in Guangzhou. The signboard over the door is attributed to 康有为; 鲁迅 ate here in 1927 and wrote it into his diary. It has been on Dianping for twenty-two years and has 45,049 reviews — a number no other Cantonese restaurant on this site comes close to.

What it sells is not nostalgia. The kitchen still hand-makes dim sum daily, the prawn dumpling is ranked third in Guangzhou, and the restaurant has appeared on the Must-Eat List for eight consecutive years and in the Michelin Guangzhou guide for five.

The menu runs from ¥24 to ¥98 with almost nothing in between that is not worth ordering: iced sweet-and-sour pork with a cold crust and hot centre, a century-old roast goose, a durian swan pastry, and a hollow sugar puff that collapses when you pick it up.

Come at 8am for the tea service, or after 5pm for the goose. The queue system is real — take a number and walk around Shangxiajiu while you wait.

What the city, the guides and the queues actually agree on.

Top 10 must-try

Dianping 推荐菜, TOP 1–10 for the Dishi Pu Road original — ranked by the app's like counts.

Source: Dianping 推荐菜 · TOP 1–10

  1. 陶陶居大虾饺
    Tao Tao Ju Prawn Dumpling ¥36 No. 1

    No. 3 prawn dumpling in Guangzhou, and the most-liked dish here by a distance — 453 likes. Wrapper thin enough to see the shrimp through.

  2. 锦绣海鲜红米肠
    Seafood Rice-Noodle Roll ¥36 No. 2

    Red rice-noodle sheet around shrimp and crisp fritter. Soft outside, shattering inside — the best texture contrast on the menu.

  3. 百搭酱皇蒸凤爪
    Steamed Chicken Feet in Supreme Sauce ¥31 No. 3

    Falls off the bone, sauce deep and savoury rather than sweet. The dim sum that tells you whether the kitchen is awake.

  4. 冰镇咕噜肉
    Iced Sweet-and-Sour Pork ¥78 No. 4

    Served over ice: the crust stays cold and crisp while the pork inside is hot. The trick dish, and it works.

  5. 百年陶陶居烧鹅
    Century Roast Goose ¥98 No. 5

    The house goose, skin roasted to a dark gold. Order the lower half if you like fat, the breast if you do not.

  6. 卡珍芝士糯米鸡
    Cheese Glutinous Rice Chicken ¥36 No. 6

    A modern addition — cheese pulling over sticky rice and chicken. Not traditional, and the table will finish it anyway.

  7. 鲜果榴莲天鹅酥
    Durian Swan Pastry ¥30 No. 7

    Swan-shaped, filled with durian. The most photographed plate in the room; order it for the person who claims not to like durian.

  8. 一口脆豆腐
    Crisp Tofu Bites ¥32 No. 8

    Silken tofu in a breadcrumb shell, fried to order. Dip in the chilli sauce, not the salt.

  9. 冰花沙翁
    Ice-Flower Sachima Puff ¥24 No. 9

    A hollow sugar-dusted puff that collapses on contact. The cheapest thing worth ordering twice.

  10. 啫啫生菜煲
    Sizzling Lettuce Claypot ¥48 No. 10

    Lettuce hit with shrimp paste in a hot claypot — the lid comes off hissing. Order it to cut the richness.

Prices from the Dianping listing. The ¥350 set feeds four for dim sum; the 100-yuan voucher sells for ¥87. Tea is charged per person on top.
