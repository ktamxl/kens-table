Ken's Table · The Accolades

The Accolades榮 譽 榜

Three things decide the top of Chinese dining: a guide built on Chinese palates, a French guide built on European ones, and — as of this year — a television show. They disagree constantly. This page explains what each one actually measures, then lists the 2026 top tier in China, city by city.

How these lists are built. No guide publishes a national 1–10 ranking. Black Pearl ranks in diamond tiers, MICHELIN ranks by city guide, and 一饭封神 eliminates until one chef is left. Each list below is the guide's own top tier in the guide's own order — filled out only where we can verify a restaurant, never padded to reach a round number.

01

Black Pearl 黑珍珠餐厅指南

Meituan · Chinese palate · 263 restaurants in 32 cities

Black Pearl was launched by Meituan in January 2018 with an explicit argument: that a Chinese restaurant should be judged by Chinese standards of cooking, not by a European guide's. It ranks in three tiers — one diamond, two diamonds, and three diamonds, which the guide calls “once in a lifetime”. It covers only Chinese cooking, and its judges are drawn from the Chinese industry.

The 2026 edition was released in Quanzhou on 27 January. It is the first edition with no Hong Kong, Macau, Taiwan or overseas cities, which is why the total fell from 370 to 263 — inside the mainland the list actually grew by seven. Of those 263: 6 three-diamond, 25 two-diamond, 232 one-diamond. Forty-six restaurants entered, seven were promoted, thirty-nine dropped, six were demoted. 2026 also introduced a service-team award, won by Xin Chang Fu in Changsha.

02

MICHELIN 米其林指南

France · European palate · three stars, by city guide

The MICHELIN Guide has been awarding stars since 1900 and has been in mainland China since 2016. The tiers are fixed: one star for high-quality cooking worth a stop, two for excellent cooking worth a detour, three for exceptional cuisine worth a journey. Unlike Black Pearl, it judges every cuisine, which is why French and Italian kitchens sit inside a Chinese city guide.

2026 was a structural year: the guides were merged into regions. The Shanghai–Jiangsu–Zhejiang guide (released 9 April in Taizhou) now covers eight cities and 409 restaurants, with 77 starred: one three-star, 14 two-star, 62 one-star. Beijing's 2026 guide lists 99 restaurants with two three-stars. Guangzhou and Shenzhen were merged into one guide in August — and neither city has a three-star restaurant.

03

The Divine Dish 一饭封神

Television · 东方卫视 · a knockout, not a guide

一饭封神 is not a guide and does not pretend to be. It is a cooking competition — a hundred chefs, split into established “big chefs” and unknown “small chefs”, cooking head to head under 谢霆锋, 张勇 and 郑永麒. Season one ran on Tencent Video in 2025 and was won by 屈雨瑜. Season two moved to 东方卫视, ran from 1 August to 1 October 2026, and ended with 邓华东, a Sichuan chef in his late sixties at 南兴园 in Shanghai, winning with a plate of mapo tofu.

The format is why it belongs on this page: no ties, no revival rounds, one dish decides. Chefs with three MICHELIN stars and chefs from street stalls go out on the same rule. It has made the reputation of cooks who were unknown eighteen months ago, and Dianping now tags their dishes with the show's name.
