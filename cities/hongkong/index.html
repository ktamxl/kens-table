City guide 城市指南

Hong Kong 香港

Roast goose, congee, dim sum and a tea-shop culture that runs from six in the morning to midnight. The densest part of this list, and the easiest city to eat well in on a budget.

Sham Shui Po alone is worth a day: 添好運, 新香園 and 合益泰 are within four blocks of each other.
