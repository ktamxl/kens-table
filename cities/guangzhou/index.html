Guangzhou 廣州

The home of Cantonese cooking, and the reason this list exists. Roast meats, river fish and a restaurant culture that rewards going early and ordering too much.

Start with 炳勝品味 on Xiancun Road, then work backwards to the older rooms in Haizhu.
