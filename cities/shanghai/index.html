City guide 城市指南

Shanghai 上海

Benbang cooking — sweet, dark, slow — served in 1920s villas and hotel dining rooms. The good rooms are small, so book before you fly.

Two very different registers: 福1088 for a private-room dinner, 人和館 for the dining room with a band.
